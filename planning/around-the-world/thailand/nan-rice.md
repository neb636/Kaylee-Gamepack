# Thailand, Nan trip, stop 3 rebuilt: Kaylee's little rice farm at Sapan (plan)

Written 2026-10-25. This replaces the toy of **Stop Time Cafe** (`rice`) in `nan-trip.md`, step 3. Read it with
`nan-trip.md` (the trip, the road, the other stops), `../ACTIVITY-TEMPLATE.md` and `docs/play-design.md`.

## Why a new toy

The first greybox (PR #168, branch `neb636/nan-rice-greybox`, **not merged**) had Kaylee's finger pour water down 13
curved terraces, draw seedlings in, then carry rain clouds. Dad played it and didn't like it. **Keep from it:** the
sky road that leads there (its end card's 🌾) and **the café deck** (pink milk, Daddy's coffee, Mommy's photo).
**Throw away:** the curved terraces, the pouring, the finger-drawn planting and the rain clouds.

Dad's picture references (Animal Crossing rice farms, not in the repo) and what we take from them:
1. Long raised paddies with thick grassy banks that have a sandy-orange cut edge, a wooden boardwalk along the front,
   a wooden stair up to a higher level, a character in a wide palm-leaf hat **standing in the field**, a banana plant.
2. **Four square paddies in a 2 x 2**, neat rows of rice hills in shiny blue water, a dirt path between them, a
   scarecrow in a straw hat, the character beside the paddies. Seen from **high and in front** (about 50° down).
3. A side view: two grassy plots with **a water channel between them**, straw bundles **drying on a rack of crossed
   poles**, a hay pile, a wheelbarrow, a plank bridge over the channel.

So: a small, tidy, cozy farm Kaylee **walks around in**, not a far valley she pours on. Chunky rectangular shapes are
also much easier to build well than curved terraces.

**And a video at the end** (Dad, 2026-10-25): [Rice Farming Explained in 60 Seconds!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qwAxh1ii-No)
(MindQuest, 60 s, 16:9): seeds in a nursery bed, planting, growing, harvest, the rice on your plate. The game follows
the same steps in the same order, so the video replays what her hands just did.

## The stop, with the template

### 3. Kaylee's rice farm at Sapan 🌾 (Nan: rice grows standing in water, from seedling to sticky rice)

**One-line pitch:** below Stop Time Cafe, Sapan's little rice farm needs planting. Kaylee wades in and plants it,
keeps the birds off while it grows, cuts the golden rice, bangs the grains out, and eats the sticky rice on the deck.

**The toy (core verb): lead Kaylee through the paddies with her finger.** Her finger held on the ground anywhere on the
farm is where Kaylee (the lilac unicorn) walks to; she follows the finger as long as it's held, and a tap walks her
there. **Whatever Kaylee walks through changes behind her**, like a lawnmower leaving a stripe: in round 1 a row of
seedlings plips in behind her, in round 2 the birds she walks near fly up, in round 3 the golden rice she walks through
falls into her arms.
- How it feels: she splashes into the water (sinks 12 cm, a splash at every step, ripple rings behind her, a
  "sploosh" step sound; on the grass banks a soft thud). Rice leans away from her as she wades and springs back after
  her. She goes up the wooden step to the back paddies with a hop. Her finger is a little ahead of her and she trots
  to catch up (fast: ~2.6 m/s on the banks, 2.2 m/s in the water, she never feels slow).
- Why it's fun with no goal: splashing a unicorn through muddy water and leaving a trail of stuff behind her is fun
  on its own (the lawnmower feeling: watch the stripe appear). She can draw shapes with the seedlings: a heart, a
  circle round the scarecrow.
- Greybox test: does steering Kaylee around with a held finger feel good on the iPad, and is the planted stripe
  satisfying? If not, change the speed, the stripe width and the sounds before anything else.

**How the verb deepens (same verb):**
1. **Plant** (`round=plant`): she carries a bundle of seedlings; where she walks in a paddy, seedlings pop in behind her
   in neat rows. Three of the four paddies start dry: tap a paddy's little wooden gate and the channel water rushes in.
2. **Grow** (`round=grow`): the rice grows by itself from young green to tall green to golden, while little birds fly
   down to peck. She walks Kaylee at them and they flap away. More birds, from more sides, as it grows.
3. **Harvest** (`round=harvest`): the water drains, the rice is golden; Kaylee holds a little sickle and the rice she
   walks through is cut into her arms, then tied into bundles that hop onto the bank. The last paddy's rice is so tall
   only her horn shows above it.

Side verb (the one): **thresh** (`round=thresh`): tap and Kaylee bangs a bundle on the threshing board, the grains
spray into the big basket. (Built in greybox B, see "What's built".)

**Her choices:** which paddy first, which gate to open, where to walk and what shape the rows make, which birds to
chase, where to cut first.

**Pokeables (5+):** the four gates (lift, gush), the scarecrow (spins, its hat flips; the birds land *on* it, they
aren't scared of it), the water buffalo in its mud wallow (moo, rolls, splashes), ducks in the channel (quack, paddle
off), a frog (hops paddy to paddy, croaks), Daddy on the boardwalk (waves, hops; splashed, he shakes like a dog), the
straw pile (Kaylee flops into it, straw puffs up), the nursery bed (seedlings wave), the café up on the hill (its wind
chime), the farm hut's chime.

**Silly moments (instead of failure):**
- Planting in a **dry** paddy: the seedlings go in **droopy and yellow** and the gate wiggles. When she opens the gate,
  the water rushes in and they all spring up straight and green with a sparkle wave. (This *is* the lesson: rice
  needs water. Nothing is lost.)
- Walk into Daddy: a big splash, he shakes like a dog. In round 3 she drops the armful of straw on his head (a straw
  hat).
- Walk into the buffalo's wallow: both of them get mud spots, the buffalo moos happily.
- Leave birds pecking: they get **rounder** (a fat-bird joke). A fat bird she shoos waddle-hops away before it flies.
- Walk her in circles: the seedlings stay in that pattern through the grow round (a heart of rice).

**Story frame (≤ 2 lines before her hands are busy):** Sparkle: "Let's plant rice, Kaylee! Walk in the water." The
first paddy is already flooded, so her first touch plants at once.
Sparkle speaks during play only when:
- she plants in a dry paddy for the first time: "Rice likes water! Open the gate." (and the gate glows)
- round 2 begins: "The birds want the rice! Shoo, shoo!"
- round 3 begins: "It's golden! Time to cut the rice."
- thresh begins: "Bang the rice! The grains fall out."
- stuck ~8 s: the 👆 on what to do next (no line, or the round's line once more)

**What it teaches, by doing:**
- Rice grows **standing in water** (she floods the paddies; droopy seedlings stand up when the water comes).
- Rice starts as **seedlings from a nursery bed** and is planted in **rows** (she carries the bundles from the
  nursery).
- **Birds love rice** (she shoos them; scarecrows are what farmers use, even if these birds sit on it).
- When it's ready the rice turns **golden** and the water is **let out** before cutting (the drain at round 3).
- **Cut, dry, bang the grains out** (threshing, `fad khao`, banging bundles on a board, as northern Thai farmers do).
- In Nan, people eat **sticky rice** (ข้าวเหนียว, khao niao) from a little bamboo basket (a **kratip**), with
  their fingers.
Tap-to-hear facts (`NAN_FACTS`, on the deck and the stamp screen), already in `nan-trip.md`'s list: "Rice grows standing
in water." "The steps on the hill keep the water for the rice." New: "In Nan, people eat sticky rice with their
fingers."

**Payoff (visible, builds up):** brown dry squares → shiny water → neat green rows → tall green → golden → stubble and
a row of bundles on the drying rack → a heaped basket of grains → the steaming kratip on the deck, then Mommy's photo
and the video.

**Replay:** the patterns she plants stay her own; birds come from random sides; which paddy is the extra-tall one
changes; ducks and the frog start in different places.

**Numbers:**

| Round | Play | Touches | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| plant | 35-50 s | 12-18 | 3 gate taps + ~3 strokes a paddy; a paddy over 70% planted fills in the rest |
| grow | 35-45 s | 15-25 | 3 waves of birds (2, 3, then 5 from two sides); growth never stops, birds only slow it |
| harvest | 30-45 s | 10-15 | ~3 strokes a paddy; over 75% cut, the rest falls by itself; 8 bundles |
| thresh | 20-30 s | 8-10 | 5 bundles x 2 bangs fills the basket |
| deck | 20-30 s | 3-6 | sticky rice, pink milk, Mommy's photo (as #168) |
| video | 60 s, skippable | 0-1 | |
| **Total** | **~2.5-3.5 min** + the video | **~50-70** | 1 line before the first touch |

Talk before the first touch under 3 s; nothing to touch for more than ~2 s only in the short beats between rounds.

   ┌──────────┐  ║  ┌──────────┐   hut 🛖
   │ paddy C  │  ║  │ paddy D  │   bamboo pipe from the hill → the channel (║)
   └────┬─────┘  ║  └─────┬────┘

   └──────────┘  ║  └──────────┘

- **Paddies:** 4, each 3.6 m wide x 2.6 m deep of water inside. Banks 0.4 m wide on top, 0.25 m above the water: grass
  green top, a sandy-orange cut side (refs 1-3). Water level 0.1 m below the bank top; dry paddies are cracked light
  brown mud (a darker crack pattern drawn as a few thin dark strips on the floor; no textures).
- **Rice hills:** a grid in each paddy, 6 across x 4 deep, 0.55 m apart = 24 per paddy, **96 in all**. One
  `InstancedMesh` for all of them (a clump of 5 blades merged into one geometry); per-hill state in a typed array.
- **The channel:** 0.5 m wide, between the left and right paddies, fed by a bamboo pipe off the hill at the back,
  running under the boardwalk bridge at the front. Water from `engine/water.ts`'s look, a gentle flow (scrolling
  glints).
- **The gates:** one small wooden board per paddy where it meets the channel (on the bank, inside the safe zone).
  Paddy A starts flooded with its gate up.
- **The step:** the back pair sits 0.45 m higher (Sapan's terraces in miniature, ref 1). A short wooden stair in the
  middle of the path; Kaylee hops up and down it anywhere along the edge (one `hopTo`).
- **Around:** the boardwalk along the front (Daddy stands on it), the nursery bed (a small bright green patch of
  seedlings, front left), the drying rack (two crossed-pole A-frames and a pole, front right), the threshing corner (a
  big round woven basket with a slatted board leaning in it) and the straw pile beside the rack, the farm hut (four
  posts, a palm-thatch roof, a chime) at the back left, a banana plant, bamboo, the buffalo's mud wallow on the right,
  the hill behind with the café deck, far mountains with cloud on the tops.
- **Walkable area:** one rectangle around the four paddies, the path and the boardwalk, with circles pushed out for
  the scarecrow, the rack, the basket and the hut. No nav grid: she walks in a straight line to the finger, sliding
  along any circle she meets. The channel is shallow: she splashes through it.

## How each round works (for the builder)

**Leading Kaylee (all rounds):** pointerdown on the canvas: first `pickAt` for pokes (gates, scarecrow, buffalo,
Daddy, ducks, frog, straw pile, chime); otherwise raycast an invisible pick mesh of the farm's ground (the paddies at
their two heights, the banks, the boardwalk) for the target. While the finger is held, the target follows it; Kaylee
walks toward it every frame (`Avatar.walk([target])` refreshed at ~10 Hz is enough, or a small `Avatar.steer(x, z)`
if that's smoother; check `characters/avatar.ts`). Let go: she stops where she is. **Birds are not tap targets**:
a tap on a bird walks Kaylee there, which shoos it. In the water: `root.position.y` drops 0.12 m, splash particles at
each footfall (`engine/particles.ts`), a ripple ring every ~0.3 m. Rice hills within 0.6 m lean away from her (a tilt
in their instance matrix), springing back over ~0.5 s.

**The brush:** every frame, hills whose centre is within **0.5 m** of Kaylee get the round's action. That's about a
2-3 hill wide stripe. When a paddy passes the threshold (70% planted, 75% cut) the rest of it fills in by itself in a
fast ripple (plip-plip-plip, rising notes) and a bell.

**plant:** she holds a seedling bundle (a green tuft parented to her arm pivot, as the mushroom farm parents the hose,
`mushrooms/farm3d.ts` ~816). A hill under the brush pops in (scale 0 → 1.2 → 1 in 0.25 s, a tiny splash, a "plip"
whose note climbs per paddy, `sounds.note`). In a dry paddy it pops in droopy and yellow-green; when the water reaches
it, it straightens and greens with a sparkle (staggered by distance from the gate). Tapping a gate: it lifts (creak),
water gushes in as particles and the water plane rises from the floor to full in 1.5 s, muddy brown → shiny sky blue.
Walking into a closed gate also opens it. Paddy A (front left) starts flooded so the first touch plants. All four
planted: a run of bells, a 2 s beat (the sun comes over the hill), then `grow`.

**grow:** a global growth value 0 → 1 over ~40 s at a base rate, sped up a little for each bird shooed (+3%), so it
always finishes. Three looks blended by growth: young (short, light green), tall (dark green, sways in the wind),
heads (drooping heads, green → gold). Birds: small round brown munias with white bellies (two spheres, a beak, two
flat wings), flying in from an edge in a little arc, landing on a hill and pecking (the hill twitches). A bird sits
until Kaylee comes within **1.3 m**: it flaps up (flutter sound, three feathers, a cheep) and flies to the hut roof, the
wire or the **scarecrow's arms**, and comes back later. Waves: 2 birds at 0%, 3 at 30%, 5 from two sides at 60%. A
bird left 6 s gets rounder (scale up to 1.4). At ~50% a little cloud passes and rains for 3 s (automatic, Kaylee
shakes off). At 100% all golden: bells, the gates turn and the water drains out to the channel (water plane sinks,
damp darker mud), then `harvest`.

**harvest:** she holds the sickle (a curved grey blade on a pink handle) and walks through golden rice: a hill under
the brush is cut (a "swish", the hill swaps to short stubble, a few straw bits fly) and her armful grows (a straw
bundle in her other arm, bigger every 4 hills). Every **12 hills cut**, she ties a bundle: it pops out of her arms with
a bounce onto the nearest bank (counter: 8 bundles in all). The **extra-tall paddy** (random, never paddy A) grows its
rice to 1.6x: in it only her horn and ears show above the waving gold, and two hidden birds burst out. All cut: the
bundles hop one by one onto the drying rack (thunk, thunk, 2 s), then `thresh`.

**thresh:** the camera moves close on the threshing corner (the rack with 8 bundles, the basket and board, the straw
pile, Kaylee beside the basket). Each tap anywhere (or on the rack, the basket, the board) is one bang: Kaylee takes
the next bundle if her hands are empty, swings it up and bangs it down on the board (anticipation up, a fast down,
"THWACK", a burst of ~30 golden grains spraying into the basket, the grain heap rises). Two bangs empty a bundle; she
tosses the straw onto the straw pile (it grows). After 5 bundles the basket is heaped and glows (the other 3 stay on
the rack). A short auto beat: the basket shakes, a little puff of chaff blows away, the grains turn white, and it's a
steaming kratip of sticky rice. The camera glides up to the deck.

**deck:** #168's deck, unchanged, plus the **kratip on the high table** next to the pink milk: tap it and Kaylee takes a
ball of sticky rice and eats it ("nom", a grain stuck on her cheek for a moment). Mommy glows after Kaylee has eaten
or sipped; tap her for the photo (as #168). Then **the video** (below), then the end card (🔁 the farm again, 🚙 the 3
road home).

**Stuck hints (👆 after 5 s with nothing touched, as the mushroom farm):** plant: the nearest unplanted spot in a
flooded paddy (or a dry paddy's gate); grow: the bird that has sat longest; harvest: the nearest uncut gold; thresh:
the next bundle; deck: the kratip, then Mommy.
