# 🏁 The Nan Mountain Cup: a kart race in the mountains

Full loop built, on for Kaylee · Nan trip stop · updated 2026-11-09

At the end of the city road, Nan town's market square becomes the race ground for the **Nan Mountain Cup**. Kaylee drives her own pink kart against three mountain racers on three tracks, each one different: a misty mushroom forest, the rice steps and the climb into the clouds. She wins two races and loses one. Which one she loses is rolled fresh every time she plays. Winning two makes her the champion, so the Cup with KAYLEE on it is hers.

**Next:** Kaylee plays it (check-in 3): [the board](#/world/thailand/nan-trip/cup) is the way in, or the city road from the Nan map. The user hears the rivals' voices first (the table read on the review page). The kart still has its first-guess feel numbers ([the feel lab](#/world/thailand/nan-trip/race?track=steps&feel=1)); her playtest says whether they need tuning.

- tracks, any order: 3
- rolled each time: 2 wins, 1 loss
- per race, ~6 min in all: ~1.5 min
- touches per race: 25-40
- rivals with a story: 3

## 🛒 Why a race here

The city road used to end at Wat Phumin. That stop was dropped (no temples), and the road was changed to end at the market, but nothing was ever built there, so the car pulls in and goes straight back to the map. The Cup fills that spot: the market square becomes the race ground, and Nan town becomes a real fourth stop with its own stamp.

> **Losing a race goes against design rule 2 (“no failing, ever”)**
>
> the user asked for one loss on purpose, so the plan makes losing **kind and a little bit special**:
> 
> - She always comes **second by a nose**, never last. The rivals hold back when she's far behind.
> - The winner is kind. They give her a **gift that goes on her kart** for the next races (a ribbon, a feather, a honey-pot sticker), so losing visibly gets her something.
> - She still gets a **silver medal**, the race's stamp counts, and the next race is open straight away.
> - The **Cup** is still hers, because two wins out of three makes her champion.
> 
> The lesson is being a good sport, and it's something she *does*: on the podium she taps the winner to give them a high five.

## 🗺️ The shape of it

Getting there: 🗺️ Tap Nan town on the map → 🏙️ The city road (already built) → 🏁 The race ground (the market square, dressed for the Cup)

The Cup (any order): 🍄 Mushroom Forest (Moon's home track) → 🌾 Rice Steps Rally (Grandma Taeng's home track) → ☁️ Cloud Top Climb (Pao's home track)

After each race: 🥇 Podium: gold, or silver + a gift → 📋 Back to the board (the next track, or 🗺️ the map)

After the third: 🏆 The Cup ceremony (KAYLEE on the Cup) → 📸 Photo for the album → 💗 Stamp on the map

> **Which race she loses**
>
> When she first gets to the race ground, the Cup rolls which track she'll lose (`lose=forest|steps|cloud`) and saves it. Next time she plays the trip, it rolls again. On that track the home rival wins by a nose; she wins the other two. It's never the **last** race she runs (user feedback, 2026-10-27), so the Cup always comes right after a win: if she won the first race and the roll is the track she hasn't picked yet, the loss moves to the one she's racing now. So the roll stops being random once one race is done and won.

## 🏎️ The toy

> **Core verb: hold a finger on the road and the kart races there**
>
> The same verb as the boat race in Krabi, which she already knows. Her finger held on the road ahead sends her pink kart there at full speed: her finger picks where across the road, and the kart always goes forward along the track. Let go and it keeps racing by itself, a bit slower, so she can never stop or get lost. On an iPhone she tilts to steer and holds a gas pedal (the boat race's `tilt.ts` and `GasPedal`). Off the road she bumps along the grass, and a hay bale boings her back. It's never a crash.

### How it deepens (same verb, every race)

1. **Just drive**
   The start straight, wide and gentle. A finger already down at “Go!” gives a rocket start.
2. **Pick a line**
   Gold coins to steer through (a little faster, up to ten), arrow pads for a burst of speed, a short cut across the inside of a bend.
3. **The track's own trick**
   Each track has one thing only it does: bouncy mushrooms, puddles that splash, the cloud. See the tracks below.
🎀. **Side verb: tap your kart**
   Before the start she taps her kart to honk, or to wiggle the gift a rival gave her. That's all.

### Things to poke (on the grid and in the race)

- 🐢 **the rivals**: hop and say their line
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Mommy and Daddy**: in the grandstand, they cheer
- 🚦 **the start lights**: beep
- 🏳️ **the checkered flag**: waves
- 🎈 **balloons on the arch**: one floats off
- 🐓 **a hen at the track side**: flaps
- 🍯 **Moon's honey stall**: a bee buzzes round

### Silly moments (instead of failing)

- 🌾 **Off the road**: the kart bumps through the grass, and a hay bale boings it back
- 💥 **Bump a rival**: both bounce, and they say “Hey!” with a laugh
- 🔁 **Wrong way**: she can't go backwards: the kart does a little spin and faces forward again
- 🐌 **Lets go for ages**: the kart cruises on and the rivals wait just ahead, waving

## 🛣️ The three tracks

Each one is its own look, its own trick and its own home rival. Each is one point-to-point run of about 60-90 s, not laps, so every race is a journey somewhere new.

- 🍄 **Mushroom Forest** (easy · Moon's home): 
  - Through the misty pine forest near the mushroom farm: a long winding loop, dappled light, fallen leaves that swirl up behind the karts.
  - **The trick: bouncy mushrooms.** Giant pink mushrooms on the track are jump pads. Drive over one and she goes *boing* up into the air, with coins to grab up there.
  - A hollow log tunnel, a stream to splash across, fireflies in the dark bit under the big trees.
  - Finish: out of the trees into a sunny clearing, with balloons on the arch.
- 🌾 **Rice Steps Rally** (middle · Grandma Taeng's home): 
  - Down the terraces at Sapan, zig-zagging from step to step under Stop Time Cafe's deck, where people cheer.
  - **The trick: puddles.** Driving through a flooded corner of a paddy sends up a big splash and muddies the kart. Muddier is a little faster, and a little waterfall near the end washes it clean.
  - A wobbly bamboo bridge, ducks crossing in a line (everyone waits a moment), scarecrows that wave as she passes, a buffalo in the next field.
  - Finish: the bottom of the valley by the river.
- ☁️ **Cloud Top Climb** (hardest · Pao's home): 
  - Up Doi Phu Kha's hairpins, the tightest bends of the three, with round mirrors on the blind bends.
  - **The trick: the cloud.** Halfway up, the track goes into the cloud and everything turns white. Glowing lanterns show the way, and her finger follows them. Then out the top into sunshine above a sea of clouds.
  - Pink **Chomphu Phu Kha** blossoms (a tree that only grows on this mountain) drift across the road. Driving through a shower of them is a burst of speed.
  - Finish: the summit, with a ring of flags and the sea of clouds below.

> **Getting harder**
>
> Easiest to hardest is forest, then steps, then cloud: wider roads in the forest, tighter bends and more to steer round in the cloud. She can race them in any order. The rivals' script keeps every race close, so a hard track is never a struggle.

## 🐾 The rivals

Three Nan locals built on the chibi rig, each with a kart that suits them, a way of driving and one short story she hears a bit at a time. These are new characters, not family.

- 🐢 **Grandma Taeng, the tortoise** (the champion): 
  - She has a rice farm on the Sapan steps and has won the Nan Mountain Cup **three years running**. Her wooden kart was built by her husband long ago. She keeps it polished, with a little parasol on top.
  - How she drives: slow off the start, then steady and never gives up. She catches up on the bends: “Slow and steady!”
  - If she wins: she ties her **lucky pink ribbon** on Kaylee's kart. “Now you're lucky too.”
  - Silly bit: she naps on the grid and wakes up at “Go!”
- 🐦 **Pao, the hornbill** (the mountain postman): 
  - He carries letters up and down Doi Phu Kha every day on a little orange kart with a mailbag, so he knows every bend by heart. A bit of a show-off.
  - How he drives: fast on the hairpins, takes the inside line, toots his bicycle bell when he passes her.
  - If he wins: he gives her **one of his feathers** for her helmet. “Special delivery, for you!”
  - Silly bit: letters fly out of his bag on the bumps, and he grabs them back.
- 🐻 **Moon, the sun bear** (the honey seller): 
  - She sells forest honey at Nan's market (her stall is still on the race ground). She races to bring honey to the people at the top. She's big, strong and happy.
  - How she drives: very fast on straights and slow on bends, and sometimes stops to sniff a flower.
  - If she wins: a **honey-pot sticker** on Kaylee's kart, and a big bear hug.
  - Silly bit: a bee follows her kart the whole race, and she swats at it.

> **How she hears their stories (talk short)**
>
> No speeches. Each rival says **one line** on the grid when she taps them (who they are), **one line** reacting in the race (passing, bumping, Moon's bee), and **one line** on the podium. More of their story shows than is said: Taeng's parasol and old kart, Pao's mailbag, Moon's honey stall and bee. The Cup's board shows their pictures and past trophies (Taeng's three cups), and a tap on each picture plays one line.

## 📖 Story and the finish

- **Arriving (at most 2 lines):** Daddy: “Look! A race!” Sparkle: “Want to race? Pick a track!” Then the board, with three track pictures and the rivals. Her finger is busy straight away.
- **The grid:** Sparkle counts the start in Thai, “Sam! Song! Neung! Go!”, so she learns 3-2-1 in Thai. A finger down at Go is a rocket start.
- **A win:** in slow motion over the line by a nose, then fireworks and the crowd jumping. On the podium she's on 1 with a gold medal, the home rival on 2.
- **The loss:** the home rival edges ahead on the last stretch, a photo finish in slow motion, and they win by a nose. “So close!” On the podium she's on 2 with a silver medal. She taps the winner to give a high five, and they give her their gift. Sparkle: “Second place! Next race!”
- **The Cup:** after the third race, the scoreboard counts 2 wins and Kaylee is champion. She lifts the big gold **Nan Mountain Cup** with KAYLEE on it, with all three rivals cheering and the gifts from the lost race on her kart. A photo for the trip album, then the Cup goes in her Trophy Room. Then the stamp drops on Nan town on the map.

2f. `#/world/thailand/nan-trip/race??track=cloud&result=lose` The Cloud Top Climb she loses: Pao wins by a nose

### New params

| param | default | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| `track` | `forest` | which track: `forest`, `steps`, `cloud` |
| `lose` | rolled | the track she loses (`forest|steps|cloud`); a link pins it, otherwise it's rolled when she gets to the race ground and saved with the trip (a new trip rolls again). Never the last race: it moves to her second race if it would be |
| `cup` | empty | races done, in order, and her place in each, e.g. `forest:1,steps:2`. On the podium it includes that race; the ceremony's link with none is the whole Cup, `forest:1,steps:2,cloud:1` |
| `place` | `1` | the podium's place, 1 (gold) or 2 (silver) |
| `kart` | `pink` | her kart's color, if she gets to pick (open question) |
| `result` | `win` | greybox 1: how the race ends, `win` (she crosses first) or `lose` (the home rival edges past and wins by a nose). From the board it comes from the roll (`cup/rules.ts` `resultFor`) |
| `cup=1` | on | the Cup is on (`CUP_OPEN = true` in `cup/rules.ts` since the full loop; set it false to hide it again, and then any `cup` value turns it on for the trip): `1` before any race, then the record, which the map, the roads and the stops carry along with `lose` while it's on; a link that starts in one of the Cup's sections turns it on too |
