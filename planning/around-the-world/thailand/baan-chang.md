# Thailand: Baan Chang Cafe, the elephants in Krabi (plan)

Written 2026-10-24. A second Krabi place on the boat trip (`boat-trip.md`), from the family's real visit and Dad's
photos. Baan Chang was one of Dad's first four Thailand picks (2026-10-17). A 2D greybox was built in PR #86 (closed,
commit `bd04281`) before Thailand moved to 3D; this plan brings it back in 3D, in the house's look (toon bands, ink
outlines, blob shadows), as part of the trip. Read it with `../thailand.md`, `boat-trip.md` (the trip kit and the
bay), `../ACTIVITY-TEMPLATE.md` and `docs/play-design.md`.

**One-line pitch:** in Krabi the songthaew stops at a signpost, and Kaylee picks the elephant. She has her photo
taken at the big bamboo sign and makes a chocolate milk. Then she walks the boardwalk down through the palms to Mali,
Khanom and Lamyai, and feeds them bananas and sugarcane from Daddy's basket until they're so happy they splash
everyone under a rainbow.

## Dad's answers (2026-10-24)

1. **Where:** her choice at Krabi. After landing she picks 🐘 Baan Chang or 🚤 the pier, in any order, and does both
   before flying home. 🐘 is also a replay shortcut, like 🚤.
2. **Food:** sugarcane and bananas, from Daddy's woven basket.
3. **Besides feeding, all four:** the photo at the sign wall, walking the raised boardwalk, a drink at the cafe, and
   an elephant splash finale.
4. **The elephants:** three, as in the photos, with fun Thai nicknames, the way people and elephants in Thailand get
   them:
   - **Mali** (jasmine): the big one.
   - **Khanom** (snack): the young one, always hungry, who sneaks sips of her chocolate milk.
   - **Lamyai** (longan, a sweet little fruit): the one further back, who wanders over later.

## What's really there (from Dad's 10 photos, kept out of this public repo)

- **The photo spot at the entrance:**
  - A curved wall of upright bamboo poles under a thick thatched band. Big white letters along it read
    "Baan Chang Café", with "We hope to be the reason you smile today" smaller underneath.
  - It stands on a wooden plank deck, with tree-stump stools, a twisted driftwood tree, and carved black-and-white
    totem posts with zigzag and spiral patterns.
  - Potted palms, ferns and a green plastic basket finish it.
- **The raised boardwalk:**
  - Dark steel posts and handrails with mesh panels, and steps going down.
  - It runs over sandy ground through a grid of tall oil palms with drooping fronds, with wooden sala huts on stilts
    in the distance.
- **The feeding yard:**
  - The ground is grey gravel. Big mossy boulders sit in a row in front of a horizontal steel pipe rail on posts.
    Cut grass and palm fronds lie on the ground, and there is a little blue bench.
  - A big elephant and a young one stand at the rail with their trunks reaching over it, and a third one is further
    back by a sala.
  - Daddy holds a woven bamboo basket of food. Kaylee, in a white cap and her pink backpack, holds a short stick out
    and a trunk curls round it.
- **In the game the elephants have no ropes or chains.** The rail and the boulders are the only line between them and
  the family.

## The toy (core verb)

**Hold food out to a trunk.** She pulls a banana or a stick of sugarcane out of Daddy's basket and holds it up over the
rail.
- **How it feels:**
  - Every trunk in reach snakes after her finger. The trunk lags a little and bends along its length, and its tip
    sniffs and wiggles.
  - The first tip to touch the food curls round it, and her finger lets go by itself. The trunk swings it up to the
    mouth, the elephant munches with a crunch, its ears flap and its belly gets a little rounder.
  - Food held high makes the trunks stretch up; food held low makes them reach down along the boulders.
  - Tapping a food (no drag) also works: it floats to the hungriest trunk, so a tap never does nothing.
  - Food dropped on the gravel (let go far from any trunk) is snuffled up from the ground.
- **Why it's fun with no goal:** a big animal follows your finger like a snake, and grabs. That's PR #86's toy, and
  the reason to build it first in 3D.
- **Greybox test:** toon shapes (a grey body, head and ears, and a trunk of ten tapered segments), the rail, three
  boulders, the basket with ten foods. Is waving a banana at Mali fun on the iPad?

## How it deepens (three steps, the same verb)

1. **Bananas and sugarcane (`feed`):** Mali and Khanom at the rail, any food to any elephant. Khanom is quicker and
   reaches for everything; Mali is slow and stretches further.
2. **Favorites (`favorites`):** each elephant shows a picture bubble of what it wants.
   - Mali wants 3 sugarcanes in a row; the count shows as three pips in her bubble ("One! Two! Three!").
   - Khanom wants bananas.
   - A wrong snack gets sniffed, held up, and passed over to the elephant who wanted it, with a happy rumble from
     both. Nothing ever fails.
3. **Lamyai and the sneaky sips (`lamyai`):**
   - Lamyai wanders over from the sala at the back (her footsteps make the basket hop), so there are three trunks
     at the rail.
   - Whenever Kaylee's hands are still for ~3 s, Khanom's trunk sneaks across to her chocolate milk on the rail
     post ("Hey! That's your chocolate milk!"). A banana held out turns him back.
   - So she keeps feeding one while she watches another. It's never a fail: if Khanom gets a sip he hiccups, and
     the milk goes down a little.
   - After 12 foods here the elephants are full.

**Side verb (the only one): pour her chocolate milk (`drinks`).**
- She pours the milk, squeezes in the chocolate, stirs, and picks a straw.
- Daddy's iced coffee is an optional second glass (ice cubes and the pot).
- It reuses the mall's Cafe & Cake props and liquid, and the overfill is a silly fountain ("Whoa! Too full!").
- The glass rides along to the yard, where Khanom wants it.

**Kept short, never a new mechanic:**
- **The sign photo (`sign`):** the bay's pose buttons and the camera flash, "One, two, three, cheese!", the photo drops
  into the album. About 15 s.
- **The boardwalk (`boardwalk`):** the house's tap-to-walk (or hold and she follows the finger) down the mesh-railed
  walkway and its steps. About 15 s, with something to poke on the way.

## Things to poke (each reacts and none of them is "the answer")

- **The elephants:** a tap flaps the ears and gets a trumpet. A tap on the trunk makes it sniff Kaylee's mane
  (she giggles).
- **Daddy's basket:** it wobbles and a banana peeks out.
- **The boulders:** a lizard pops up.
- **The sala huts:** a myna bird flies out.
- **Along the boardwalk:** a gecko on a post calls "to-kay", and a tap shakes the palms so a frond floats down.
- **The entrance:** the totem posts give a woody "tock".
- **The blue bench:** Daddy sits down for a second ("Ahh").

## Silly moments (instead of failure)

- A banana held over Khanom's head: he stands up on his back legs a little and wobbles.
- Food waved round and round makes all the trunks go round in circles, then they bonk together (boing) and sneeze.
- Khanom gets a sip of the chocolate milk and has a chocolate moustache on his trunk tip.
- Too much food too fast: Mali burps a big rumbly burp, and Daddy laughs.

## Payoff (it builds while she plays)

- The bellies round out and the ears flap faster as the elephants fill up.
- **The splash (`splash`):**
  - Full and happy, the three dip their trunks in a water trough and spray up into the air. The water rains over
    the family, a rainbow arcs over the yard, Kaylee squeals, Mommy's hat comes off, and Daddy's sunglasses drip.
  - Each tap on an elephant gets another spray.
  - It's a different look from the race's Elephant Beach: up into the air and down on the family, not an arc over
    the water.
- **The photo and the sticker:** Daddy's photo of the splash (the elephants, the rainbow, the family) drops into the
  trip album, and can go on Agong's wall. A **baby elephant sticker** (Khanom) flies into the album, the 10th animal
  to find on the trip.
- **What comes next:** a big 🚤 (to the pier) if she hasn't been to the bay yet, otherwise ✈️🏠 (home).

## Where it plugs into the boat trip (verified in the code, 2026-10-24)

**It's part of the activity `boat-trip`, not its own activity.**
- `BoatTrip.tsx` mounts one world at a time and swaps worlds behind the boarding pass. It also holds what all of
  this needs: her outfit (`packed`), the album, `photos` and `found`, the single stamp at `home`, and
  `useSaved('thailand-boat-trip')`.
- The boat race (`race/`, with `RaceLayer.tsx` on top) is the model: a world of its own inside the trip.
- Baan Chang adds the world `'chang'` to `TripWorld`, `WORLD_OF` (`trip.ts`) and `ANCHORS_OF`.

**Hidden until it's ready.**
- `meta.ts` `inTheWorks` can't hide sections of a live activity, so a `CHANG_OPEN` flag goes in a small data file
  with no three.js, and `?chang=1` forces it on.
- While it's off, the fork and the 🐘 choices don't show (the songthaew goes straight to the pier as today), but the
  section links work.
- The full-loop PR turns it on.

**The fork at Krabi.**
- `trip/legs/krabi.ts` `buildArrival()` gets a signpost down the road with two painted boards, an elephant and a
  longtail (pictures, no words).
- In `trip/legs/plane.ts` `stepKrabi()`, the songthaew pulls up at the signpost instead of going on to the pier.
- Two new tap targets in `PLANE_ANCHORS`, `sign-chang` and `sign-pier`: a tap turns the songthaew that way, toots,
  and the leg ends with `done('chang')` or `done('pier')`.
- The fork shows only while neither place is done. ⏩ waits at the fork.

**What she has done.**
- A new param `been` (`chang`, `bay`) says which Krabi places are done. It is not `stops`, which the bay reads for
  its own glows (`village`, `cave`, `race`).
- `Progress` gains `been`, and the `home` leg clears it.
- `RESUME` and `RESUME_AT` gain `chang` (every Baan Chang section resumes at `chang`).
- `PASS` gets `{ id: 'chang', icon: '🐘', label: 'Elephants' }` before 🏠.
- `legOf()` maps the chang world to `chang`.
- `doneBefore()` adds what `been` says, because with two orders the pass's index order no longer tells what's
  punched.
- `docked()` punches 🐘 only if `been` has it.

**Routing.**
- **At the pier at sunset:** the ✈️🏠 button gets a big pulsing 🐘 beside it while she hasn't been to Baan Chang
  (`finish('chang')`), and Sparkle's idle line becomes "The elephants are waiting!".
- **After the splash:** `ChangLayer` shows 🚤 (`finish('pier')`) if `bay` isn't in `been`, otherwise ✈️🏠
  (`finish('home', null)`).
- **On a replay:** `yes()` offers 🐘 next to 🧳 and 🚤, and `choose()` gets a `'chang'` case.

## Tech plan

New folder `activities/boat-trip/chang/`, in the style of `trip/legs/` and `race/`:

```
chang/
  chang.ts        class Chang extends Stage (trip/legs/stage.ts): the world, its sections, CHANG_ANCHORS, qa()
  layout.ts       meters, pure data: the sign deck, the cafe, the boardwalk's floor, the rail, boulders, elephant spots
  yard.ts         the gravel, rail, boulders, salas, palms, trough; sign.ts the bamboo wall; cafe.ts the sala and counter
  elephant.ts     the rig: body, head and ear pivots (from race/wildlife.ts buildElephant, copied now, shared later),
                  face life, the trunk, munch, ear flap, trumpet, spray, the belly's roundness
  trunk.ts        PR #86's FABRIK chain (makeTrunk, stepTrunk) ported from 2D points to Vector3
  feel.ts         the tuned numbers from PR #86 (notice, grab, tip chase, curl, munch), in meters
  basket.ts       Daddy's woven basket and the foods
  ChangLayer.tsx  the favorite bubbles and the 🚤 / ✈️🏠 buttons
```

**What it reuses:**
- **The family and the stage:** `makeFamily` and the shots in `Stage`.
- **The pour:** `buildCup`, `buildStraw` and `buildStrawPot` (`mall-trip/games/cafe/props.ts`) and `liquid.ts`.
- **The walk:** `Walker` (`trip/walk.ts`) on one raised `Floor`.
- **The splash:** `RAINBOW` (`race/scenery.ts`) and the particles (`engine/particles.ts`).
- **The photos:** `snapPhoto` and `photoCamera` (`engine/photo.ts`).
- **The poses:** the bay's `PoseButtons`.

**The trunk** (the riskiest piece):
- Each segment is one static tapered cylinder plus a knuckle sphere. Every frame each one is turned toward its
  FABRIK point with `quaternion.setFromUnitVectors`, so no geometry is rebuilt per frame and the ink outline still
  works.
- The tip chases its target with lag (her food, the mouth, the milk, the trough, the sky). Droop pulls the chain
  down when nothing interests it, and a slow wiggle keeps it alive.
- Who gets a food: the nearest tip within reach whose elephant isn't munching. With favorites on, the elephant who
  wants it reaches a little faster.

**The album, the photo fan and Agong's wall:**
- `PhotoKind` (`bay/part.ts`) gains `'chang'`. The compiler then flags `Album.tsx` `FACT` and `PhotoFan.tsx`
  `ORDER`, the two places to add it.
- `CRITTERS` (`bay/critters.ts`) gains `'elephant'`, with its art in `Stickers.tsx`. With 10 stickers the album's
  3-column grid gets a fourth row; check it looks right.
- The `bay`-only checks around `Album`, `PoseButtons`, `PhotoDrop`, `StickerFlight` and the `enter` effect in
  `BoatTrip.tsx` widen to cover `chang`.
- Agong's wall needs no change: it hangs whatever photo she picks.

**No WebGL2 (`Flat.tsx`):**
- After `land`, a fork page with 🐘 and 🚤 cards.
- A page for each Baan Chang section: tap 🍌 or 🎋 and the elephant munches, then the splash and its 🚤 or ✈️🏠.
- Every section link lands on its page.

**QA:**
- `__thaiBoatQA()` gains `fed`, `holding` and `trunks`, read-only.
- `scripts/qa-screens.mjs` gets the new section ids.
- `qa-motion.mjs` gets the trunk following a held banana, the grab and munch, Khanom's sneaky sip, and the splash.
- `qa-pacing.mjs` gets every new section.
- `tests/sections.spec.ts` and the tap-around find the sections by themselves, so there's no bespoke spec.

**Performance budget:** under ~200 draw calls and ~200k triangles in the yard, at 60 fps on the iPad. The palms are
instanced, and the elephants are a few thousand triangles each.

**Riskiest pieces, prototyped first (greybox 1):**
1. The 3D trunk following a finger and grabbing, so it feels alive and not robotic.
2. Holding food in the right place on every screen shape (the plane in front of the rail, reachable in portrait and
   on iPhone).
3. Two trunks after one food: who gets it has to look fair and readable.
