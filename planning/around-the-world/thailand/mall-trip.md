# Thailand: Mall Day at ICONSIAM (plan)

Written 2026-10-20, revised 2026-10-21 from Dad's photos. Thailand's second family trip. Mommy, Daddy and Kaylee
spend a day at a Bangkok mall: they play at HarborLand and then eat at The Pizza Company. It's built in 3D in the house's look (toon bands, ink outlines, blob
shadows) and reuses the boat trip's trip kit. Read it with `../thailand.md`, `boat-trip.md` (the trip kit),
`../ACTIVITY-TEMPLATE.md` and `docs/play-design.md`.

**One-line pitch:** Mommy drives the silver rental car to ICONSIAM by the river. Kaylee puts on her HarborLand socks
and plays her way round a giant indoor playground: she's a vet in Harbor Town, gives Daddy a crazy haircut, wins the
racing slide, climbs the Big Frame and slides down a dragon, until she's hungry. Then she shares a stretchy-cheese
pizza fairly. The two slices left over go home to Agong in the box, and the big cousins finally look up from their
phones.

## What's really there (so the game feels like the place)

**MEGA HarborLand ICONSIAM** (6th floor, next to the cinema; opened 23 Dec 2021; for ages about 5 months to 15 years):
- **The theme:** "Little Thailand: have fun in one place, travel all over Thailand" (สนุกที่เดียว เที่ยวได้ทั่วไทย).
- **Named rides:**
  - the **Dragon Spiral Tower Slide**, twin dragons round a spiral tower
  - the **Boomerang Donut Slide**, where you ride a ring that swings up and back
  - the **Ta-Khon Roller Slide**, named after Phi Ta Khon, Loei's masked ghost festival, and HarborLand's longest roller slide
  - a low **zip line**
  - **Interactive Ball Attack** and the **Interactive Cannon Target**
- **Thai decor:** Lopburi monkeys, Phi Ta Khon figures, and a **Golden Mountain** to climb. The Golden Mountain is most likely Wat
  Saket's Golden Mount; that's my guess, not a source.
- **Also:** multi-level climbing nets, a ball pool, a bicycle machine where parents race their kids, and the Sky Pod (a wall of
  light-up buttons with a score).
- **Zones:** Toys Room and Little Ville for the small ones, Kids Island (101-140 cm), Junior Jungle (120 cm and up), JumpZ
  trampolines next door, a whole floor of inflatables, and a Kids Cinema with ball pits.
- **The way in:** shoes go on free racks. **HarborLand grip socks are a must** for everyone (฿60), and kids are
  **measured at the counter** because the price depends on height. Play lasts 3 hours, and grown-ups come in on a parent ticket.

### The mini games (Dad picks the order; ★ = my first three)

Each follows `../ACTIVITY-TEMPLATE.md` in short: one core verb that deepens three times, about 20-40 touches and
1.5-3 minutes, at most one or two spoken lines before her hands are busy, and no failing.

#### ★ 1. The Vet Clinic 🩺 (`vet`; caring, body parts)

From Dad's photos: blue scrubs and cap, a plush pony and a golden retriever on the table, a tray of vet tools.

- **Pitch:** a puppy limps in with a sore paw. Dr. Kaylee (in her scrubs and cap) makes every patient better.
- **The toy:** grab a tool from the tray and use it on the patient. It works right where she holds it:
  - the stethoscope: thump-thump hearts float up, loudest over the chest
  - the thermometer: the patient's cheeks glow red, then cool
  - tweezers, cream and bandages: she drags a bandage round and it wraps
  - the brush: the fur fluffs up
- **How it deepens:**
  1. **The puppy's check-up:** the stethoscope, the thermometer, then the sore paw: pull out the thorn, cream, bandage.
  2. **The muddy pony:** bath time. Soap suds appear where she rubs, the shower rinses them, the towel dries, the
     brush fluffs the mane, and a bow goes on top.
  3. **A baby elephant** (Thailand's animal) with a sniffly trunk: medicine on a spoon, a giant tissue. The sneeze blows
     the suds everywhere, then the elephant gives her a happy trunk shower.
- **Her choices:** the bandage's color, the bow, which patient comes in next.
- **Pokeables:**
  - a cat in its carrier that meows back
  - a parrot that copies her sounds
  - the x-ray box (the puppy's bones wag)
  - the treat jar
  - the scale (the patient sits and the needle wiggles)
- **Silly:** the stethoscope on Daddy (BA-BOOM, he hams it up); the thermometer in the pony's ear.
- **Payoff:** each healed patient hops off the table and joins a happy row in the waiting room, wagging.
- **Tech:** a 3D exam room in the house's look, with the tools as 3D props dragged by the finger (like the airport's
  suitcase). Tap zones on the patient: head, chest, paw, tail. New toy animals: a puppy and a pony (`characters/looks.ts`
  has no dog or horse yet); the elephant exists. **Riskiest:** a tool landing on the right part of a 3D animal, every
  time, on an iPhone too.

#### ★ 2. The Barber Salon 💇 (`barber`; silly, shapes)

- **Pitch:** Daddy wants a new look before lunch.
- **The toy:** tools on Daddy's mane:
  - the grow tonic: squirt it and the mane grows long and wild
  - the scissors: snip where she drags, and tufts fly
  - the comb: drag to make spikes, or smooth it down
  - the dryer: big poof
  - color spray: rainbow stripes
  - clips and bows
- **How it deepens:**
  1. Grow and snip. Anything goes.
  2. Colors and the dryer.
  3. A kid customer shows a picture card (spiky, pigtails, curly). Whatever she makes, she gets a laugh and
     "close enough!".
- **Payoff:** the chair spins round to the mirror for the reveal. Daddy loves it and poses, and Mommy takes a photo.
  **He keeps the hairdo for the rest of the trip**, at the pizza table and at home, where Agong laughs (param `hair`).
- **Silly:**
  - snip his horn and it boings back
  - tonic on his eyebrows
  - the dryer on full blows everyone's mane
- **Tech:** the mane as a grid of strands (one instanced mesh), each strand with its own length and color; the
  scissors cut the strands under her finger. **Riskiest:** strands that read as hair in the toon look.

#### ★ 3. The Racing Slide 🏁 (`race`; counting down, taking turns)

From Dad's photo: four wavy orange lanes into pink and white balls.

- **The toy:** climb the steps (tap, tap), sit at the top in lane 1 with Daddy, Mommy and two kids beside her.
  3-2-1, and she taps to scoot faster over each bump. Whee into the balls.
- **How it deepens:**
  1. The first race.
  2. Tap just as she hits a bump and she flies higher.
  3. Race in rubber rings, spinning.
- **Payoff:** a winner's ribbon per race (she always wins by a nose). Daddy lands in the balls head first.
- (A race is also the boat trip's big ride. This one is a quick playground race: no laps, no items.)

#### 4. Cafe & Cake ☕ (`cafe`; colors, mixing, Thai drinks)

From Dad's photos: a white-tiled kitchen, a coffee machine, a blender, breads in baskets, a tea set, a cake stand.

- **The toy:** the blender. Drop fruit in (mango, strawberry, watermelon, banana, coconut), press the button
  (whirrrr, the colors swirl), then tilt to pour into a cup, with a straw and whipped cream on top.
- **How it deepens:**
  1. Blend anything; every color is right.
  2. Orders in picture bubbles ("pink!": strawberry and banana).
  3. Thai drinks: **cha yen** (orange Thai tea poured over ice with milk), a coconut shake. Two orders at once.
- **Pokeables:**
  - the fridge (bottles clink)
  - the coffee machine (it steams: Daddy's coffee)
  - the bread baskets (the pretzels bounce)
  - the cake stand (it spins)
  - the tea set (pour Mommy a cup)
- **Silly:** forget the lid and the blender splats the ceiling; Daddy gets brain freeze.
- **Not cake cutting:** cutting and sharing slices is the pizza's toy.

#### 5. The Golden Mountain ⛰️ (`mountain`; counting, colors)

From Dad's photo: a big yellow cone with climbing holds and Thai patterns, a red rail round the top, a shiny steel slide
straight down the front, and kids riding elephants painted on the wall behind it. Filled in with
`../ACTIVITY-TEMPLATE.md` on 2026-10-21, before the greybox.

**One-line pitch:** the Lopburi monkeys sit on top of the Golden Mountain ringing its bell; Kaylee climbs up hold by
hold, counting, rings the bell herself and zooms down the steel slide, then beats Daddy to the top.

**The toy (core verb):** grab the next hold and pull herself up to it.
- How it feels: every hold she can reach from where she hangs glows. Her finger goes down on one and her hand reaches
  for it at once; pull the finger down and she pulls herself up with it (a tap does the whole pull). She stretches, rises,
  lands with a squash, her legs dangle and swing, and the hold's number pops up big over it with a note one step up the
  scale (1, 2, 3...). Over the top she hops onto the platform, turns round and cheers.
- Why it's fun with no goal: going up, and then the big slide down, is the playground's oldest loop; the slide is the
  payoff she wants again.
- Greybox test: the cone, round holds, the bell, the steel slide, Kaylee, Daddy and three monkeys in the toon look. Does
  pulling up feel good on the iPad (tap and pull-down), and does the slide make her want to go again?

**How the verb deepens (3 steps, same verb):**
1. **Holds in a line:** five holds straight up her side; count them to the top, ring the bell, slide.
2. **"Only the pink ones!":** six rows of mixed colors; the pink ones in reach glow, and she picks her own way (two or
   three pinks a row, left or right). A non-pink hold is a silly toy, never an "oops": the yellow one honks like a
   clown nose, the blue one giggles and wobbles, the mint one is a monkey's hiding place (a monkey leaps out beside it,
   blows a raspberry and leaps back). She stays put and the pinks keep glowing.
3. **Race Daddy up the other side:** seven rows on her side, seven on his. He climbs when she does, puffing (puff clouds,
   huff huff), and waits for her, panting, if she stops. **She always wins, just:** he's always one hold behind, and he
   flops over the rail as she rings the bell. Then he follows her down the slide and lands head first on the mat, legs
   kicking.
Side verb: none (the bell and the slide are the climb's payoff: one tap each).

**Her choices:** which hold next (round 1 has one; rounds 2 and 3 have two or three in reach), left or right, ring the
bell or go straight down the slide, poke the monkeys on the way up.

**Pokeables (5+ things that react and aren't the answer):**
- the monkey at the foot (a backflip and a chatter)
- the monkey on its ledge halfway up (scratches its head, swings its tail)
- the monkey on the top rail (swings right round the rail)
- the bell (rings any time; from the floor the top monkey rings it for her)
- the painted elephants on the wall (a trunk goes up and sprays a fan of water drops, a trumpet)
- the steel slide (a shine runs down it, ting)
- the Thai pattern bands on the cone (gold ripples round them)
- Kaylee (a giggle and a wiggle, even on the wall) and Daddy (a hop and a laugh)

**Silly moments (instead of failure):** the non-pink holds in round 2 (honk, giggle, monkey); the bell rung from the
floor (the monkey rings it and covers its ears); Daddy's head-first landing; a tap on a hold out of reach makes it
wiggle and the ones in reach pulse brighter.

**Story frame (at most 2 lines before her hands are busy):** Sparkle: "Climb the Golden Mountain, Kaylee! Count the
holds!" (with the full loop; the greybox has no voices).
When Sparkle talks during play: when round 2 starts ("Only the pink ones!"), when Daddy steps up ("Race Daddy to the
top!"), and after ~8 s with nothing touched (the 👆 shows the next hold, the bell or the slide).
Counting (with the full loop): each hold's number spoken as it pops ("One!" ... "Seven!"), in Sparkle's voice.
Daddy's lines (full loop): "I'm going to win!" (round 3 starts), "Huff... puff..." (while he climbs), "You beat me,
Kaylee!" (at the top), "Whoa-oa-oa!" (his head-first landing).
Kaylee at the top: "I did it!" Other lines: "Ding dong!" (Sparkle, the first bell), "Wheee!" (Kaylee on the slide).

**What it teaches, by doing:** counting up as she climbs (one hold, one number, one note up the scale), picking out a
color (only the pink ones), and that a temple sits on top of the Golden Mount. Tap-to-hear fact (full loop, on the bell
the first time and the stamp): "The real Golden Mount in Bangkok is a temple on a hill. People climb over 300 steps to
its golden top!" (Sources disagree on the exact count: 344 steps spiralling round the hill per
[Thai Holiday Guide](https://www.thaiholidayguide.com/attraction/wat-saket-golden-mount/), 318 per
[Penang Travel Tips](https://www.penang-traveltips.com/thailand/bangkok/golden-mount.htm); "over 300" is true for all of
them.) Lopburi's monkeys are real too: a whole town in Thailand full of monkeys.

**Payoff (visible, builds up while she plays):** a flag on the top rail for each round (pink, pink, then a gold one with a
star for beating Daddy), the bell's sparkles, the slide. The ⛰️ sticker goes on her sock band the first time she
reaches the top.

**Replay:** she picks a different route each time in rounds 2 and 3; after the third round it stays open as a toy
(race Daddy again, as often as she likes; the 🛝 takes her back to the hall).

**Numbers:** about 30-35 touches: round 1 about 7 (5 pulls, bell, slide), round 2 about 9-11 (6 pulls, a silly hold or
two, bell, slide), round 3 about 9 (7 pulls, bell, slide), plus pokes. About 2 min. No lines before the first touch in the
greybox; one with the full loop.

**Camera and art:** a 3D side view, straight on and a little above, in the house's toon look. The camera follows her up
the mountain (she stays big on an iPhone) and pulls back for the slide.

**Screens:** wide and tall framings; the holds she can reach (one row), the bell and the slide's mouth stay inside the
safe zone, under the top bar.

**Sections and state:** one section, `mountain?round=1|2|3` (the shared `round` param, with its own round state in
