# Thailand: Mall Day at ICONSIAM (plan)

Written 2026-10-20. Thailand's second family trip. Mommy, Daddy and Kaylee spend a day at a Bangkok mall: they play at
HarborLand and then eat at The Pizza Company. It's built in 3D in the house's look (toon bands, ink outlines, blob
shadows) and reuses the boat trip's trip kit. Read it with `../thailand.md`, `boat-trip.md` (the trip kit),
`../ACTIVITY-TEMPLATE.md` and `docs/play-design.md`.

**One-line pitch:** Mommy drives the silver rental car to ICONSIAM by the river. Kaylee puts on her HarborLand socks,
climbs a soft-play "Little Thailand" (the Golden Mountain, the Ta-Khon roller slide, the twin-dragon spiral slide),
fires the ball cannon at Daddy until she's hungry, and then shares a stretchy-cheese pizza fairly. The two slices left
over go home to Agong in the box, and the big cousins finally look up from their phones.

**Dad's picks (2026-10-20):**
- **ICONSIAM's MEGA HarborLand** ("Little Thailand"), chosen from the options below.
- **Just Mommy, Daddy and Kaylee.**
- **Mommy drives a rental Toyota Veloz, in silver.**
- Later the same day: the sticker is **a pizza slice**, the real names can be used, and there are no photos to work from.
- At the pizza table, **pull and share** (not make-a-pizza: Italy's pizzeria already does that).

## Choosing the mall (research, 2026-10-20)

HarborLand has 30 indoor play parks in Thailand. These were the candidates in and around Bangkok:

| One Bangkok (6F, Nov 2024) | 6,000+ m² | Onederland (Himmapan forest) | Harbor Skyscraper (6 levels, 12 m), Sky Monorail, LED spider tower | Not found |

| Gateway Ekamai | 4,000 m² | Sweet Paradise (candy) | | Not verified |

ICONSIAM won because its theme *is* Thailand. Every ride is a Thai thing (dragons, the Phi Ta Khon ghost masks, the
Lopburi monkeys, a Golden Mountain), so the playground teaches a little about the country while she plays. There's no
Pizza Company in ICONSIAM, so the game puts one next to HarborLand on the 6th floor (Dad, 2026-10-20: it can go anywhere).

## What's really there (so the game feels like the place)

**MEGA HarborLand ICONSIAM** (6th floor, next to the cinema; opened 23 Dec 2021; for ages about 5 months to 15 years):
- **The theme:** "Little Thailand: have fun in one place, travel all over Thailand" (สนุกที่เดียว เที่ยวได้ทั่วไทย).
- **Named rides:**
  - the **Dragon Spiral Tower Slide**, twin dragons round a spiral tower
  - the **Boomerang Donut Slide**, where you ride a ring that swings up and back
  - the **Ta-Khon Roller Slide**, named after Phi Ta Khon, Loei's masked ghost festival, and HarborLand's longest roller slide
  - a low **zip line**
  - **Interactive Ball Attack** and the **Interactive Cannon Target**
- **Thai decor:** Lopburi monkeys, Phi Ta Khon figures, and a **Golden Mountain** to climb. The Golden Mountain is most likely Wat
  Saket's Golden Mount; that's my guess, not a source.
- **Also:** multi-level climbing nets, a ball pool, a bicycle machine where parents race their kids, and the Sky Pod (a wall of
  light-up buttons with a score).
- **Zones:** Toys Room and Little Ville for the small ones, Kids Island (101-140 cm), Junior Jungle (120 cm and up), JumpZ
  trampolines next door, a whole floor of inflatables, and a Kids Cinema with ball pits.
- **The way in:** shoes go on free racks. **HarborLand grip socks are a must** for everyone (฿60), and kids are
  **measured at the counter** because the price depends on height. Play lasts 3 hours, and grown-ups come in on a parent ticket.

Things nobody wrote down, so they're our design: the colors, the heights and the layout. Dad has no photos of it
(2026-10-20), so the greybox builds the playground from the ride names and descriptions above, and his iPad test
decides the rest.

**ICONSIAM:**
- It sits on the Chao Phraya river.
- Its glass front zig-zags like *baisri*, banana leaves folded into offerings.
- **Alangkarn**, on the same 6th floor, has a 15 m indoor waterfall with a light show.
- SookSiam, on the ground floor, is an indoor floating market with food cooked on wooden boats.
- A fountain show runs along the river at night.

**The Pizza Company:**
- Thailand's biggest pizza chain (Minor Food).
- Its 2023 look is a fresh "Iconic Green" with a logo whose A is a pizza slice. Its delivery number is 1112.
- Real menu: Hawaiian, Seafood Cocktail, Tom Yum Kung, Double Cheese, and the "Extreme" crust stuffed with sausage and cheese.
- The drinks are Coca-Cola, so the game shows a plain soda (no other brands). The Pizza Company's name is shown and
  spoken (Dad, 2026-10-20); its sign is our own toon drawing in its green, not a copy of the logo.

**The car:** a Toyota Veloz (Thailand, 2022 on), a 7-seat MPV.
- Hinged rear doors, roof rails, and a full-width LED light bar across the tailgate.
- Right-hand drive, so Mommy sits on the right, as in Aunty Sri's van.
- Rental companies in Bangkok do have it.

## Starting: Mommy asks where to

Once there are two trips, Mommy's card (`trip/legs/Ask.tsx`) asks **"Kaylee, where shall we go?"**. The 👍 Yes button
becomes two picture buttons:
- 🚤 for the boat trip
- 🛝🍕 for the mall

## Goodbye 👋 and the silver Veloz 🚗

- The family who live here gather at the shutter, and she can hug anyone she taps.
- **Agong: "Bring Agong something yummy!"** That's the trip's goal, and the pizza box pays it off.
- Gan, Ken and BB wave without looking up from their phones.
- The silver Veloz is parked at the curb. Mommy gets in on the right (she drives), Daddy in front beside her, Kaylee in
  the back by the window. The doors swing shut, *thunk, thunk*, and it pulls away. That's the whole drive.

## Arrive at ICONSIAM 🏬 (one quick glimpse, then the HarborLand counter)

1. **A 3-second glimpse,** a cut: the Veloz pulls up in front of the zig-zag glass front with the river behind it.
2. **The lift:** she taps the big button and the lift dings up, *ding 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6!*. Sparkle counts along; it's
   the only counting before the pizza.
3. **The HarborLand counter.** These are tiny toys, not chores:
   - **Shoes off:** tap the rack and Kaylee's shoes hop into a cubby. Daddy's big shoes don't fit, and he squashes them in.
   - **Socks on:** three pairs of grip socks (pink, lavender, mint). She picks one and Kaylee wears it from now on
     (`socks`). Daddy's are too small: they stretch up to his knees.
   - **The height chart:** Kaylee stands against it, and the staff member says "Wow, so tall!" because **her horn
     counts**. Daddy stands there too, and his horn goes off the top.

## HarborLand: Little Thailand 🛝 (the main event)

**The toy:** climb up, slide down.
- She walks Kaylee around the way she does in the house: tap a spot and she goes, or hold a finger and she follows it.
- Climb nets and the Golden Mountain link the levels; when the path crosses one, she climbs it by herself.
- Slides only go one way: tap a slide's mouth and she sits, whooshes down a curve, and lands in balls that splash up.
- Greybox test: boxes for the levels, nets as ramps, slides as tubes, the pool as a box of balls.

**How the verb deepens** (three levels, any order, nothing locked):
1. **The ground floor.**
   - The **ball pool**: dive in and balls fly; tap the balls to toss them.
   - A little slide.
   - The **Lopburi monkeys** on the wall: tap one and it steals Daddy's sock and runs off with it.
2. **The Golden Mountain** (climb it up to the second level).
   - The rope bridge wobbles; tap it to bounce.
   - The **Boomerang Donut**: she sits in the ring, it swings up the wall, back down and up the other side.
   - The low **zip line**: hold on and whoosh across.
3. **The top.**
   - The **Ta-Khon roller slide**: long and bumpy, *brrrrr*, and the Phi Ta Khon masks along the side pull faces as she rattles past.
   - The **twin Dragon Spiral Tower**: the longest whoosh, round and round between two dragons, with the camera following her down into the big pool.
   - Mommy takes the photo for Agong's wall as she shoots out.

**Side verb (only one): the Cannon Target.** Tap the cannon and a ball flies in an arc. Daddy is the target: *bonk*, he
topples into the pool and pops up with a ball stuck on his horn. Ball Attack is the same game with more balls.

**Things to poke:**
- the Sky Pod buttons (they light up and beep a tune)
- the bicycle race machine (Daddy pedals, puffing, and Kaylee always wins)
- the Phi Ta Khon figures (they jiggle their bells)
- the monkeys
- a crawl tube where Daddy gets stuck (his horn bonks the top, as in the cave)
- Mommy waving from the parents' bench
- the other kids playing around (sounds and animation, no voices)

**The build-up:** a little sock-band card in the corner fills with a sticker for each big ride (the ball pool, the
mountain, the donut, the zip line, the roller slide, the dragon).

**Silly moments:**
- Slide head first and she pops out of the balls upside down.
- Hit Daddy three times and he cannonballs into the pool himself.
- Tap a monkey while it holds Daddy's sock and it puts the sock on its head.

**When it ends (she chooses):** after the dragon slide, or after about 3 minutes, Daddy's tummy rumbles, *grrrrumble*,
and Mommy asks **"Hungry? Pizza time?"** with 🍕 Let's eat and 🛝 More play. More play gives her another minute and then asks again.

## The Pizza Company 🍕 (pull and share)

The camera looks across the table from the side, at eye level, with the family on the far side. The green sign and the
window onto Alangkarn's waterfall are behind them.

**The menu:** three big pictures she can tap, no reading: **Hawaiian 🍍**, **Seafood 🦐** or **Double Cheese 🧀**. Double
Cheese has the longest stretch. The server brings the one she picks on a stand (`pizza`).

**The toy:** pull a slice.
- Drag a slice off the pan and the cheese stretches long and thin, wobbles, and snaps back, *boing*.
- Drop it on a plate.
- Tap a slice on a plate and that person munches it, *chomp*, with cheese on their chin.
- Greybox test: a disc cut into 8 wedges, a stretchy band between the slice and the pan, and three plates.

**How the verb deepens:**
1. **The first pull.** The stretch alone is the wow.
2. **Share fairly.** There are 8 slices and 3 plates. One for Mommy, one for Daddy, one for Kaylee, and round again; Sparkle
   counts each plate out loud. The plate that's behind glows. Nothing is wrong: give Daddy three and he says "Kaylee,
   Mommy's hungry too!". With 2 each, two slices are left: **"Those are for Agong!"** She pulls them into the box, and
   the lid closes, *flup*.
3. **The cheese tug.** Daddy grabs the other end of a slice: the longest cheese pull across the whole table, and it
   snaps onto his nose.

**Things to poke:**
- the soda glass and straw (slurp, bubbles)
- the chili shaker (Daddy sneezes)
- the server (she bows, "Khob khun ka!")
- the onion rings (Mommy wears one as a bracelet)
- the napkin box (a flurry)
- the green sign (it glows)
- the waterfall through the window (it changes color)

**Learning by doing:** sharing fairly (the same number for everyone, counted round the table) and counting to 8. The
leftovers are a gift, not a mistake.
