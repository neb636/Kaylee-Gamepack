## 🧭 What's really there

So the game feels like the place.

- 🏝️ **Koh Samet**: A long, thin island about **7 km north to south and 3 km across**, a 10-minute speedboat (or 40-minute ferry) from **Ban Phe** pier on the Rayong coast, about three hours' drive from Bangkok. It's a **national park** (Khao Laem Ya–Mu Ko Samet, since 1981), and up to 80% of it is forest. Nearly every beach is on the **east** side (sunrise); the west is mostly rocky, with **Ao Prao** its one real beach (sunset).
- 🌳 **Named after a tree**: *Samet* is the **cajeput tree** (*Melaleuca*), with soft, white, papery bark. In the old poem the island is *Ko Kaew Phitsadan*, the “Magic Crystal Island”.
- ✨ **Hat Sai Kaew, “crystal sand”**: The main beach: fine white sand, beach chairs and umbrellas, **jet skis**, banana boats, and **fire shows every night** at the beach bars. On the rocks at its south end the **mermaid and her child** statue looks out to sea.
- 🧜 **The mermaid's story**: The poet **Sunthorn Phu** (about 200 years ago) wrote *Phra Aphai Mani*, a poem so long it took him about 20 years. A prince plays a **magic flute**, a giant of the sea keeps him under the waves, and a **mermaid** helps him escape to this island. Thailand's most famous story-poem, and the reason Samet has mermaids.
- 🌙 **Bays with lovely names**: *Ao* means bay. **Ao Prao** is Coconut Bay, **Ao Wong Duan** is Crescent Moon Bay (it's moon-shaped), **Ao Phai** is Bamboo Bay, **Ao Thian** is Candlelight Bay.
- 🚕 **Getting around**: Narrow concrete and dirt roads, few cars: **songthaews** (green pickup taxis with benches in the back), motorbikes and ATVs. Everyone walks between the beaches on sandy paths over the rocks.
- ☀️ **Weather**: One of the **driest, sunniest places in Thailand**, hot all year, with only a short rainy season. There's no river: fresh water comes from a reservoir in the middle of the island.
- 🤿 **The little islands**: Snorkel boats go out to the small islands east of Samet: **Ko Kudi** (a beach and a reef), **Ko Khang Khao**, “Bat Island” (coral), and **Ko Kham**, joined to its neighbor by a sandbar at low tide. Parrotfish, blue damselfish and cuttlefish; branching, brain, plate and soft corals; sea urchins in the shallows (watch your feet).

> **🙏 Left out: temples, Buddhas, shrines (AGENTS.md rule 7)**
>
> Samet has **Wat Ko Samet** with a 7 m white Buddha right on the road from Na Dan to Sai Kaew, a **wayside shrine beside the mermaid statue**, the **crab shrine** (Chao Pho Pu Dam) at Na Dan and another shrine near Sai Kaew, and a hill named *Khao Phra Chedi*. None of them go in the game: that stretch of road is just trees, the mermaid stands alone on her rocks, and we never use the hill's name. On the mainland only the pier appears (Ban Phe has temples and spirit houses too).

> **👹 Also left out: the sea giant**
>
> A big bronze statue of the poem's sea ogress (*Phi Suea Samut*) stands at the end of Na Dan pier. She's the story's baddie and a bit scary for a five-year-old, so the pier has no statue; the mermaid tells the story.

**How the numbers were made**

Each place's lat/lon (OSM, via the Overpass API) is turned into meters from Sai Kaew (1° lat = 110.6 km, 1° lon = 108.6 km at 12.57° N) and divided by 10. The playable island is then about **210 m across and 240 m long** (x -200…+10, z -95…+150) plus the sea round it. The coastline is traced from OSM's outline (lat 12.5217–12.5830, lon 101.4418–101.4702) at the same scale and smoothed, the hills exaggerated so the island has a spine to walk over. These numbers go in `island/layout.ts` as data.

## 🏝️ The island: roam anywhere

`island`; the hub, and the toy that holds the trip together.

> **👆 Core verb: lead Kaylee with her finger**
>
> The way she already walks in Agong's house and the rice farm: **hold a finger and Kaylee trots after it**, hold it far ahead and she **gallops**, tap a spot and she walks there. Walk into the sea and she's **swimming in her pink floatie**, bobbing over the waves. Walk up to the green taxi, the jet ski or the snorkel boat and they glow: tap to hop on.

### How it feels

- **Sand is soft:** her hooves leave prints, she sinks a little and kicks up puffs; on the wet sand by the water she leaves shiny prints the waves wipe away.
- **The sea is bouncy:** in the floatie each wave lifts her and drops her (a little *whee* the first times), a big one tips her into a spin. She paddles where her finger is, slower than walking.
- **Paths over the rocks:** between the beaches a sandy path climbs over the round granite boulders through the trees, with steps; the camera swings round so the next beach shows ahead.
- **Mommy and Daddy come along:** they follow at a little distance (Daddy's “keep up” from the HarborLand hall, generalized) and stop to do their own thing at each beach: Mommy takes photos, Daddy lies on a beach chair (tap him: he jumps up).

### How it deepens (it's a toy, so the island opens up as she plays)

1. **Land and wander**
   Na Dan pier and Sai Kaew. The sand, the sea, the floatie, the first animals, the green taxi waiting. Sparkle's one line: “Let's explore the island!”
2. **Further: the paths and the green taxi**
   The paths over the rocks to Hin Khok, Phai and Wong Duan; the **green taxi** (a songthaew: tap to climb in the back, it bounces along the concrete road over the hill and stops at the next beach; she can hop off at any stop) is the way to Ao Prao on the west.
3. **The jet ski is hers**
   After the jet ski stamp, a jet ski waits at the water's edge on **every** beach: hop on and ride the coast to another beach (the fast, fun way round). The open world gets bigger because of something she did.
🐚. **Side verb: the beach clean**
   Bits of rubbish wash up (a bottle, a bag, a flip-flop). Pick one up and drop it in the beach bin: plonk, and an animal pops out where it was (a hermit crab climbs out of the bottle, a fish jumps). Teaches: it's a national park, we keep it clean. Never required.

### Things to poke (the island has dozens; these are the regulars)

- 🥥 **Coconut palms**: bonk, a coconut drops and rolls
- 🌳 **Samet trees**: papery bark peels off and flutters
- ⛱️ **Pink umbrellas**: spin, one flies off down the beach
- 🪑 **Beach chairs**: fold up with Daddy in them
- 🧜 **The mermaid statue**: a magic-flute tune, the mermaid's tail splashes
- 🚕 **Green taxi**: honk honk
- 🍌 **Banana boat**: whooshes past full of squealing people
- 🏐 **Beach ball**: boing, kick it into the sea
- 🍦 **Ice cream cart**: dings, a coconut ice cream
- 🛍️ **Bead seller's tray**: it jingles
- 🦀 **Crab holes**: a crab peeks out and runs sideways
- 🌊 **Waves**: splash, the foam chases her up the sand

### Silly moments

- 🌊 **Walk into a big wave**: it rolls her over in the floatie, she pops up giggling with seaweed on her horn
- 🥥 **Stand under the palm and poke it**: a coconut bonks Daddy
- ⛱️ **Poke the umbrella three times**: it takes off like a kite with Kaylee holding on, floats a little, lands
- 🚕 **Ride the taxi round and round**: the driver waves every lap and the songthaew toots a tune

### What it teaches, by doing

- Koh Samet is an **island**: walk any way and you reach the sea; the mainland is over the water.
- *Ao* means **bay**; each beach is a bay with its own name (tap-to-hear: Coconut Bay, Crescent Moon Bay, Bamboo Bay).
- **Island life**: no big cars, green taxis and walking paths; the boats are how you get anywhere.
- Tap-to-hear facts (first time she pokes it, then on the stamp screen): the samet tree, crystal sand, the mermaid's story, the national park.

> **🤫 Quiet hub**
>
> Like every hub: no idle hints spoken. After ~8 s with nothing touched, the nearest stop glows and a little pink flag-shaped arrow points to it from the edge of the screen. Sparkle only talks when something is new (the first time she reaches each beach, the jet ski becoming hers, a new animal).

## 📷 Mommy's phone and the animal book

`book`; the thread that makes her look closely at the whole island.

> **📱 Snap the animals**
>
> Mommy lends Kaylee her phone (a big pink button in the corner). Tap it and the screen becomes the phone's camera, still following Kaylee. **Tap an animal** and it's a photo: flash, click, the animal does something cute for the camera, and the photo flies into her **animal book**, filling its silhouette. ### The animals (real Samet animals; where to find them)

- 🦎 **Monitor lizard**: Waddles across the paths and under the resort deck. Flicks its tongue at the camera.
- 🐿️ **Variable squirrel**: Runs up the palms, chatters.
- 🐒 **Long-tailed macaque**: In the trees on the hill road; tries to grab Daddy's sunglasses.
- 🦌 **Mouse-deer**: The tiny deer, shy, only in the forest by the path to Ao Prao. Very rare to snap: a sparkly gold page.
- 🐦 **Oriental pied hornbill**: In the tamarind tree at Ao Wong Duan (a real sighting there). Big yellow beak.
- 🦇 **Flying fox**: The big fruit bat, only in the golden afternoon sky and at night.
- 🪶 **Pacific reef heron**: Stands on the rocks by the mermaid, fishing.
- 🕊️ **Terns**: Dive into the sea by the jet ski.
- 🦀 **Ghost crab and hermit crab**: On every beach (generic beach life, not a Samet-only record).
- 🐠 **Parrotfish**: Snorkeling at Ko Kudi, crunching the coral.
- 🔵 **Blue damselfish**: Little blue fish in swirling crowds at Ko Kudi.
- 🦑 **Cuttlefish**: Changes color when she gets close.
- 🟣 **Sea urchin**: Prickly, on the rocks: look, don't touch.
- 🪸 **Coral**: Branching, brain and plate corals (it's alive).

### The rules of the book

- **Never required.** No stamp or sticker waits on it; it's the reason to wander and to come back.
- **Some are rare on purpose:** the mouse-deer, the hornbill, the flying fox at dusk. Rare ones show a sparkle in the bushes when she's near so she can spot them.
- **Pages fill up visibly:** a page per beach, the stop's sticker in the corner of its page when the flag is earned. A full book plays a little fanfare and puts a gold star on its cover.
- **Teaches:** the island is a national park full of animals; you can enjoy them by looking and taking a photo, not by catching them.

## 🛥️ The jet ski at Sai Kaew

`jetski`; Dad's must-have, and greybox 1.

> **👆 Core verb: steer the jet ski with her finger**
>
> **Finger down anywhere on the water and the jet ski heads for it**; the longer and further she holds, the faster it goes (it never needs a button). Let go and it glides, slows and bobs. Daddy sits behind her with his hands over hers (“Whee!”), and the jet ski man pushes them off the sand, like in the photo.

### How it feels

- **Zippy and splashy**: a fast start with the nose lifting, a white rooster tail of spray behind, a lean into every turn, a little slide on tight ones. The wake stays on the water behind her as a white line.
- **It bounces over the swell**: small hops on the little waves, a real jump off a big one (the engine whines in the air), and a **big splash** on landing that sprays the camera.
- **Why it's fun with no goal:** going fast on the water and making spray is fun by itself, and the wake shows where she's been. Greybox test: a box jet ski on the toon water in a buoy rope square, the beach and the hut as blocks.

### How it deepens (same verb)

1. **Ride** (?round=ride)
   The calm bay inside the buoy rope. Carve, spray, circle the buoys, splash the swimmers (they squeal and wave), drive through the banana boat's wake. **The secret:** drive a loop and close it, and the wake circle sparkles and fills with jumping fish (a heart-shaped loop gets pink sparkles).
2. **Waves** (?round=waves)
   The wind comes up and bigger waves roll in from the open sea. Steer into them to **jump**: higher waves, bigger jumps, a spin in the air on the biggest. The terns dive beside her.
3. **Race the jet ski man** (?round=race)
   “Race you to the big buoy!” Out round the far orange buoy and back to the beach against the jet ski man. She wins most of the time (he slows behind her, he takes a wide turn), and when he wins it's by a nose: “So close! Again?” and it starts again at once. The **winner rings the bell** on the hut.
🛥️. **Then it's hers**
   After the stamp the jet ski waits on every beach: ride it round the coast to any beach (the island's fast travel, and the free ride).

### Things to poke

- 🔔 **The bell on the jet ski hut**
- 🍌 **The banana boat**: tow it, everyone falls off
- 🟠 **Buoys**: bonk, they bob and ring
- 🏊 **Swimmers**: wave, splash back
- 🐟 **Jumping fish**: follow them
- 🕊️ **Terns**: dive
- 🧔 **Daddy**: his cap blows off, he catches it

### Silly moments (instead of failure)

- 🏖️ **Drive up onto the beach**: it skids up the sand, everyone tumbles off laughing, the jet ski man pushes it back
- 🌀 **Spin in tight circles**: Daddy goes dizzy (“Wooo-ooo!”), spirals over his head
- 💦 **Splash Mommy on the shore**: she's soaked, then laughs
- 🚧 **Push through the buoy rope**: the rope stretches like a rubber band and boings her softly back

### Story, teaching, payoff

- **Story frame:** the jet ski man: “Life jacket on!” (she taps the jacket: click, it's on). Daddy: “You steer, Kaylee!” Then her hands are busy. Sparkle only when something is new (“Big waves! Jump them!”, “Race to the buoy!”) or after ~8 s with no finger down (“Touch the water to go!”).
- **Teaches by doing:** **life jacket first**, every time (it waits for the click); stay **inside the buoys** where it's safe (the rope boings her back); waves come in from the open sea. Tap-to-hear: Sai Kaew means crystal sand; the jet ski pushes water out the back to go.
- **Payoff:** the wake art she drew, the race win and the bell, the pink flag on Sai Kaew, the jet ski hers.
- **Replay:** the free ride round the island, a heart to draw, the race again (a little faster).

> **🎛️ Feel on knobs (step 1)**
>
> Steering is the thing we got wrong twice (Nan, the bay): the jet ski's top speed, acceleration, turn rate, slide, the finger's dead zone and how big a wave makes a jump are all `knobs()`, with `watch()` graphing her finger against the jet ski's heading. ## 🏰 Sandcastle at Ao Hin Khok

`sandcastle`; a little calm bay next to Sai Kaew, the swimming beach.

> **👆 Core verb: dig the sand with her finger**
>
> Drag through the sand and it digs: a trench opens under her finger and the sand piles up beside it, wet and dark. Drag along a pile and it grows into a wall. **The sand moves where she touches**, the real stuff, no tools to pick.

### How it deepens

1. **Dig** (?round=dig)
   Dig holes and moats, pile walls and hills. Kaylee (on the sand next to it) digs along with her finger, sand flying. A crab pops up out of a hole she digs.
2. **Bucket towers** (?round=towers)
   Her pink bucket (if she packed it; the beach shop lends one if not): drag it onto the sand and tap it, and it flips and lifts to leave a tower. Different moulds: round tower, square tower, a **unicorn** mould. Push shells and coral bits into the walls.
3. **The tide comes in** (?round=tide)
   The waves creep up the beach. **Dig a channel** from her moat to the sea and the water rushes in and fills it (the best moment). A hermit crab moves into a tower door. A wave too big washes one tower half away: “Oh no! Build it again!”
🧔. **Side verb: bury Daddy**
   Daddy lies down next to the castle: drag sand over him until only his head and toes stick out. Wiggle his toes (tap). He sneezes and the sand flies off.

- **Her choices:** where to dig, how big, which moulds, where the shells go, where the channel runs.
- **Pokeables:** the hermit crab, a ghost crab running sideways, the beach ball, Mommy reading under the umbrella (she peeks over her book), the ice cream cart, a boat going by, the foam.
- **Teaches:** the **tide** comes up the beach and goes down again; wet sand sticks, dry sand runs. Tap-to-hear: crabs dig holes to hide from the sun.
- **Payoff:** her castle, which stays on the beach (saved), with water in the moat and a crab living in it. Mommy's photo of it goes in the book.

## 🛠️ Tech plan: an open world on an iPad (for the builder)

Every 3D world so far is one small scene: the house (~26 × 24 m walkable), the bay (a 205 m circle, driven by boat). The island is one scene too, about **260 × 300 m with the sea**, walked at Kaylee's height. That's fine for three.js if we keep draw calls and overdraw down; it doesn't need streaming.

- ⛰️ **Terrain (new, shared)**: `engine3d/terrain.ts`: a height field from data (`island/layout.ts`: the coast polygon from OSM at 1:10, hill bumps, beach slopes), meshed once at ~1 m resolution with vertex colors (sand / grass / rock bands in the toon palette) and toon-shaded. `heightAt(x, z)` and `surfaceAt(x, z)` (`'sand' | 'wet' | 'grass' | 'rock' | 'water'`) for walking, footprints and sounds. In the engine, not Thailand, so Canada or the Poles can use it.
- 🌊 **Water (extended)**: `engine3d/water.ts` today is one flat plane with noise blotches and wave dashes. Add, behind options so the bay and the race don't change: **depth color** from a depth texture baked from the terrain (turquoise shallows, teal, deep blue), **shoreline foam** bands that slide up and back, and a real **swell** in the vertex shader that matches `swell(x, z, t)` (floatie, jet ski and boats already bob to it). Splashes and the wake stay in `Particles` (`foam`).
- 🚶 **Walking at island size**: `NavGrid` uses 20 cm cells: 1.3 M cells for the island is too many. Either a cell-size option (50 cm → ~200 k) or per-beach grids joined by the paths' polylines. Finger-follow (the common case) needs no A*: steer toward the finger, slide along obstacle circles (palms, boulders, chairs). Tap-to-walk uses A*. The RoamPad stick stays off (her finger is the control).

## The sandcastle, fixed (issue #357 sandcastle batch, 2026-11-09)

- **The tide's channel (the major one):** both directions worked for the robot; what failed was a channel started on the moat's *rim*: it left a dam of sand between the moat and the channel, the water stopped there, and the channel's own water counted as "the moat filled" (`pooled()` counted any water inland). Now the moat counts as filled only when water stands in the deep part of her biggest hollow (`Sand.hollow()`, `wetArea`). When her channel's water stops within `TIDE.help` (1.25 m) of the moat and her finger is up `helpAfter` (1.2 s), the sand between glints and gives way (`breach`, `Sand.carve`); further off, the pressing finger traces the gap still to dig (`Sand.gap`). A stroke that starts on a wall right beside a hole and runs away from it digs through the wall instead of patting it up.
- **The dig round shows progress:** each sixteenth of the way round the castle she digs twinkles a note higher, the moulds bounce in one by one (a quarter, half, three quarters), and a moat dug `PLAY.ring` (0.85) of the way round gets a fanfare, everyone cheers and Daddy says "Great castle!", then the towers. 30 s of digging still moves on by itself.
- **The towers round is the core verb:** a mould has to be full of sand before a tap flips it. Drag it through the sand and it scoops (a trench behind it), or dig beside it (within `fillNear`) and the sand arcs in. A full one heaps over and hops now and then. Each tower of the round needs more sand (`fill` 0.08 m³, `fillMore` ×0.5 each); 6 towers (or 150 s) bring the tide. Tapping an empty one wobbles it and Sparkle says "Dig next to a bucket to fill it!". Lines: `towers` reworded, `hint.fill` new (the voices job records them).
- **Every screen digs:** the patch reaches 15 m up the beach (`PATCH.v0`, `DIGGABLE.v0` −13.9); a phone in portrait shows sand down to about −13.3, which didn't dig below −7.6.
- **Daddy:** moved in to `a` 2.45 so he's whole on an iPhone in portrait (his right edge at 343 of 390 px). Rubbing heaps the sand on the parts near her finger, not just the one under it; about 6 rubs bury him (`bury` 1.6). Fully buried, he lies still, wiggles his toes and sneezes himself out after `wiggleOut` (3.2 s, paused while she's still rubbing).
- **The dark box at the channel's mouth:** the sand only got wet inside the box where she had dug. Now `Sand.soak()` wets and dries the whole patch a few times a second, fading over a few cells.
- **Robot runs:** `thailand-samet-sand-{moat,bucket,bury,out}`. `qa()` adds `moat` (core/wet m²), `ring`, `digK`, `fills`, `helped`, and `moatIn`, `moatRim`, `seaEdge` screen points.
- **Known:** the walls beside a channel still stand in spikes where it meets the sea; a mould dragged off the castle flips where it is.
