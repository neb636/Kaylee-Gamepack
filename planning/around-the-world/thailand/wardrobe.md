# 👗 Kaylee's Wardrobe: dress up her unicorn

The full loop built · Toy in Agong's house · updated 2026-10-31

Kaylee loves the avatar maker in Roblox, so she gets one in Thailand. In her room at Agong's she opens her wardrobe and dresses her unicorn herself: she drags a crown, heart sunglasses, fairy wings or light-up shoes onto herself and they pop on, and she drops paint on her dress, her hair or her fur to change its color. What she picks is saved on the iPad, and she wears it **everywhere in Thailand**: the house, every trip, every mini game and the photos on Agong's wall. When a game needs its own hat (the chef's hat, the vet's cap), the game's hat wins for that game and hers comes back afterwards.

**Next:** The user checks the restored original wardrobe on the iPad: all six drawers, everyday clothes, paint, the wand and mirror photos. Her saved everyday look and photo door are back, including on Halloween.

- drawers: hats, glasses, wings, outfits, shoes, paint: 6
- things to wear (10 hats), plus 3 colors to paint: 25
- a visit: 1-3 min
- touches a visit: 15-40
- ways in: her wardrobe, or the mirror button anywhere in the house: 2

## 💡 The idea

Written 2026-10-26, from the user: “In Roblox Kaylee really likes the avatar customizer. Can we make something similar for the 3D Thailand game, easy to go back to, saved on the iPad, so her avatar shows up everywhere?” Plus: work out every place where her own hat (or anything else) would clash with a game's costume, and solve all of them.

> **🧸 A toy, not a menu**
>
> Roblox's avatar screen is a grid of buttons; Kaylee can't read the labels, and tapping squares isn't play. Here she **touches the real things**: the wardrobe's drawers slide out, the clothes sit on its shelves, and she grabs one and puts it on her unicorn. Whatever she does gets a reaction, and nothing is wrong (a shoe dropped on her head sits on her horn until she shakes it down to her feet).

> **🏠 Not an activity**
>
> It's a toy in Agong's house, like the mattress or the fan: no passport stamp, no finale, no trophy. Its payoff is seeing herself in her own look everywhere, and the family noticing it (“Ooh, a crown, Kaylee!”).

## 👆 The toy

One verb: put it on her.

> **Core verb: put it on her**
>
> Her finger picks up a real thing from the wardrobe (a crown, wings, a pot of paint) and drops it on her unicorn. It pops on with a sparkle and she shows it off with a little pose. A quick tap on a thing works too: it flies onto her. Tap something she's wearing and it hops back to its shelf.

🛏️ Her room (tap the wardrobe) → 🚪 Doors swing open (she hops onto the rug, facing you) → 🗄️ Pull a drawer (its things pop up on the shelf) → 👑 Drag it onto her (pop! she poses) → 🎨 Paint (drop it on hair, dress or fur) → 📸 Mirror photo (the look is kept on the door) → ✨ Off she goes (and wears it everywhere)

### How the verb gets richer

1. **Things on and off**
   Hats, glasses, wings, outfits, shoes. One per spot: put a second hat on and the first hops back to its shelf with a boing. Each thing gets its own pose (the crown: a curtsy; the wings: a flutter hop; the sunglasses: a cool lean).
2. **Paint where you drop it**
   The paint drawer holds pots of color. **Where she drops the pot decides what changes**: on her hair, her hair; on her dress, her dress; on her fur, her fur. Splash, a shimmer, done. Same verb, and now it matters where her finger lets go.
3. **Whole looks**
   The mirror takes a photo of her look and pins it to the wardrobe door (up to 4). Drop a photo on her and she wears that whole look at once. Making a look, keeping it, swapping between them: the Roblox outfits, done by hand.
🔄. **Side verb: spin her round**
   Drag across her and she turns on the rug to see the back (wings! the cape!). Let go and she springs back to face you.

### Things to poke (they all react)

- 🚪 **Wardrobe doors**: creak open and shut
- 🗄️ **Drawers**: slide out, rattle, shut with a thunk

## ⚖️ When her look clashes with a game

Every clash in the code today, and the one rule (plus a few small ones) that solves them all.

> **🧩 The rule: the game's costume goes on top of her look**
>
> Her look is one layer and a game's costume is a layer on top. Each costume says which **spots** on her it needs: head, face, body, back, hands or feet. On those spots the costume wins for that game. She keeps everything else, and when she leaves the game her own things come back.
> 
> So the vet in **heart sunglasses and fairy wings** is fine (the vet only needs her head and body), but the chef's hat goes on *instead of* her crown.

> **✨ Never lost**
>
> When a costume takes a spot, her own thing doesn't vanish: it shrinks into a sparkle that pops into her pocket, and pops back out when she leaves (the same little show both ways, no words needed). A five-year-old should always see where her crown went.

### Costumes: what each game needs

| Game | Costume | Spots it needs | What she keeps |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🩺 Vet | scrubs and scrub cap | head, body (+ the stethoscope on her ears) | glasses, wings, shoes, colors |
| 🍰 Cafe | apron and chef's hat | head, body | glasses, wings, shoes, colors |
| ✂️ Barber | smock | body, back (wings would poke through it) | hat, glasses, shoes, colors |
| 🥊 Muay Thai | mongkhon, gloves, the gold belt | head (nothing goes on top of a mongkhon), face, hands, body | wings, colors (and her grip socks) |
| 🌾 Rice farm | her ngob (farmer's straw hat), mud spots | head | everything else |
| 🧦 HarborLand | grip socks | feet: her shoes hop into the cubby at the counter (that moment is already in the game) | everything else |
| ☀️ Sunny trips | what she packed (sun hat, sunglasses, floaties) | head and face, *unless her own are sun-smart* (next card) | the rest |

### The small rules

- ☀️ **1. Sun smarts**: On a sunny trip her own hat **counts if it's shady** (cap, cowboy hat, bucket hat, sun hat): she keeps it, no spare hat, and Mommy says “Good hat, Kaylee!”. A crown isn't shady, so the packed sun hat (or Mommy's spare) goes on and the crown waits in her pocket. Heart and star sunglasses count as sunglasses. The packing lesson stays exactly the same, and now her own choice can be the right one.
- 🦄 **2. Nothing taller than her horn**: Every roof in the game was made to clear her horn: the tuk-tuk, the van, the Veloz, the Pajero, the plane's bins, the sea cave's tunnel. So every hat stays under the tip of her horn, and none of those need changing. Her tap targets and the cameras, which use her height, don't change either.
- 🪑 **3. Wings fold when she sits**: In a car seat, on a stool, a couch, a booster seat, a slide or the donut ring, wings fold flat and the cape drapes; lying down (pillows, the canoe in the cave, the straw pile) they tuck away. Folded, they stay inside the circle she walks in, so she never snags on furniture. They spread when she stands still, and flap when she hops.
- 👟 **4. Shoes off in the house**: Like a real Thai home: at Agong's door her shoes hop onto the shoe rack (it's already by the door) and back on when she goes out to the street. A real culture fact she does with her feet. In the wardrobe she can still try them on, on the rug.
- ✋ **5. Her hands stay free**: No things to hold in the wardrobe (for now). Every game puts things in her hands (the stethoscope, the sickle, the key fob, the boxing gloves, the photo for Agong, the trophy), and none of them will ever fight with her look.
- 🖼️ **6. Photos remember**: A photo on Agong's wall shows what she wore that day. Changing her look later doesn't change old photos.
- 🙏 **7. Respect**: For a wai (the spirit house, the altar, the little shrine, the village's mosque dome, the wai kru in Muay Thai) her hat comes off into her hooves and goes back on after. The user's call (see below). Sacred things still never wobble.
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **8. Everyone still knows her**: She's always a unicorn and her face never changes, so the family (and Kaylee) always know who she is. A bare start (no saved look) is exactly today's Kaylee.

### Greybox 1: the wardrobe toy ([done] · 2026-10-26)

The wardrobe in her room: doors, six drawers, 12 things and 8 pots of paint; drag on, tap on, tap off, spin, paint. Saved on the iPad and worn in the house.

- **Paint first, in the lab:** `characters/model.ts` recolors her texture in the toon shader per mesh group and hue band (fur ~272° on face, arms, legs, ears and neck; dress ~322° on body and sleeves; hair ~22° and darker than 0.55 on hair, braids and tail, so the gold horn and the pink and mint streaks stay). Each texel keeps its shading. One shader program, one cached material per look. Try it in the character lab’s **Look** panel or `?look=`.
- **The look:** `characters/style.ts` (slots, items, colors, `parseLook`/`formatLook`, `currentLook()`: the link’s `look`, else the saved one). The Hub saves it with `useSaved('thailand-look')`; `look` is a sticky param and the wardrobe keeps it in step with what she wears.

### Greybox 2: everywhere, and every clash solved ([done] · 2026-10-26 · PR #205)

Her look in every scene from one place, a costume layer that takes only the spots it needs (the rest goes in her pocket), sun smarts, folding wings, shoes onto the rack at Agong's door, hat off for a wai, the ngob as a costume, and the drawn photos in her colors.

- **One place:** `characters/avatar.ts` has a `Dress` (her look + the game's costume on one rig). `Avatar` and the race's `Racer` both dress through it, and both constructors read `currentLook()`, so the 6 `new Avatar(...)` (house, trip-kit stage, bay, race, mountain road, lab) and the race slide and Sky Pod show her look with no scene changes. ## 🛠️ Tech plan and risks (for the builder)

> **🎨 Riskiest: painting a modelled toy**
>
> Her colors are painted into the GLB's one texture (a 1k JPEG atlas, one material; meshes `body_mesh`, `armL/R_mesh_new`, `legL/R_mesh`, `hair_mesh`, `face_mesh`, `ear_L/R`, `tail_mesh`, `braid*`). Colors in it, roughly: lilac fur (hue ~265°), pink dress stripes and ear insides (~330°), brown hair (~25°), cream, mint and pink streaks, gold horn.
> 
> Plan: a recolor in the toon material's shader (`onBeforeCompile`), **per mesh group** and **per hue band**: the dress recolor runs only on the body and arm meshes and only on the pink band (so the pink streaks in her hair and her pink ear insides stay); hair only on `hair_mesh` and the brown band (streaks stay); fur on face, arms, legs, ears and tail on the lilac band. Keep each texel's lightness, so the shading painted into the texture stays. One cloned material per group (about 3 more materials, shared by every Kaylee with the same look). Watch the JPEG's soft edges between bands (a soft band edge, not a hard cutoff) and the stripes. Prototype this first in greybox 1, in the lab, before building the wardrobe.

### Other risks

- **Fitting.** Each item is fitted to her measured shape like `model-wear.ts` does (the hair dome frame for hats, the face frame for glasses, `shell()` for outfits, `along()` for arms). Wide outfits (tutu, gown) change the body silhouette: the costumes that sit on the body (apron, belt) take the body spot, so they never sit inside a gown.
- **The horn rule** is a test, not a hope: a check in the lab (and a unit-ish check in the build) that every hat's bounding box tops out under `HORN × height` (1.09, `cave.ts:39`).
- **Memory.** Items are built the first time they're worn and go in the scene's `Bag` (disposed with it). The wardrobe builds the shelf items lazily per drawer.
- **The code-built Kaylee** (if the GLB can't load): colors map to `Look.fur` / `mane` / `outfit.color`; items fall back to `animal.ts`'s frames (`buildWorn`), or simply don't show.
- **Tests stay deterministic.** Test browsers start with nothing saved, so it's today's Kaylee. `tap-around` and `sections.spec.ts` find the new `wardrobe` section by themselves. No bespoke spec.
- **Voices** (gate 4): Sparkle's few lines, a name per thing (finite, ~30 short clips), and one notice line per kind of thing for Mommy, Daddy, Agong and Jum. All in `lines.ts`.
