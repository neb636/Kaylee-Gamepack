# Thailand: the Nan Road Trip (plan)

Written 2026-10-24. Thailand's third family trip, and the first that is mostly about **learning about a place**.
Mommy, Daddy and Kaylee fly north to Nan, pick up a silver rental **Mitsubishi Pajero Sport** at the airport and drive the
swoopy mountain roads between four real places: an old temple, a mushroom farm, a café over the rice terraces and a
village that makes salt from a well. It's built in 3D in the house's look (toon bands, ink outlines, blob shadows) and
reuses the trip kit. Read it with `../thailand.md`, `boat-trip.md` (the trip kit), `mall-trip.md` (a trip with many
small toys), `../ACTIVITY-TEMPLATE.md` and `docs/play-design.md`.

**One-line pitch:** Kaylee presses the key and the big Pajero beeps hello. Mommy drives and Kaylee steers it round the
bends of the mountains into the clouds. At each stop she makes something the mountains make: she wakes up an old
painting with a lantern, mists mushrooms until pink ones pop out, fills the rice steps with water, and boils salt from a
well. On the way home the road draws a giant **3**.

**One idea holds it together: the mountains make things.** Clouds, rice steps, mushrooms and salt all come from Nan's
mountains, and Kaylee makes each one with her own hands. The road between the stops is the toy that ties them together.

## What's really there (so the game feels like the place)

- **Nan** is a quiet province in the far north-east of northern Thailand, along the border with Laos. It's all
  mountains and valleys, with the Nan River running down the middle. Nan Nakhon Airport (NNT) has flights from Bangkok
  Don Mueang, and rental desks at the terminal.
- **The Pajero Sport** is a big seven-seat Mitsubishi SUV, and it's **made in Thailand** (Mitsubishi's Laem Chabang
  plant). Right-hand drive: in Thailand cars **drive on the left**.
- **Wat Phumin** (Nan city): a cross-shaped temple from long ago. It sits on the backs of two giant **naga**
  serpents, whose bodies run along the stairs, with a door on each of the four sides. Inside, **four golden Buddhas**
  sit back to back, so whichever door you come in, a Buddha faces you. The walls are covered with old murals of
  stories and of everyday life in Nan (from the 1860s-70s). The most famous one is **Pu Man Ya Man**, a man
  **whispering** into a woman's ear: Nan's "whispering lovers". Statues of the couple stand in the garden.
- **Baan Huanam Mushroom Farm** (บ้านหัวน้ำ, 129 Moo 5, Sila Laeng, Pua): a farm restaurant in the trees with a
  view of the countryside. It grows its own mushrooms, and its **mushroom pizza** is the dish everyone orders.
  Oyster mushrooms in Thailand grow from **bags of sawdust** that hang or stand in rows in dark, damp, misted
  houses. They love the rainy season. **Pink oyster mushrooms** are a real kind (*Pleurotus djamor*). Whether
  Baan Huanam grows them is unconfirmed; if not, they're our one made-up touch, because Kaylee loves pink.
- **Stop Time Cafe** (photos 2-5): a café on a hill above **Sapan village** (สะปัน) in Bo Kluea district, with a
  wooden balcony looking over the village's rice terraces and the mountains. In the rainy season the fields are green
  and the clouds sit low; in the cool months it looks over a sea of fog. Sapan sits where two rivers meet, with a
  three-tier waterfall nearby. In June the paddies are flooded and freshly planted: rice grows standing in water,
  and the steps hold the water on the hillside.
- **Bo Kluea** (บ่อเกลือ, "salt well"): a mountain village near Laos where people have made salt for hundreds of years
  (local stories say about 800). They lower a bucket down the well, pull up very salty water, pour it into wide pans
  over wood fires and boil it for hours until only white salt is left, then scrape it into baskets. The salt comes from
  a layer of rock salt left by **a sea that was here long, long ago**. (The salt huts close in the full rainy season,
  from Buddhist Lent in July; the family was there just before, in June.)
- **The "number 3" road** (ถนนเลข 3): Route 1081 between Santi Suk and Bo Kluea. From the viewpoint, its hairpin bends
  look like a giant number 3 drawn on the hill. Route 1256 over **Doi Phu Kha** (the "sky road") climbs into the cloud
  between Pua and Bo Kluea.
- **The roads** have chevron signs on the bends, steel guard rails, round **convex mirrors** on blind bends, and
  drivers give a short **beep** before a blind bend. Water buffalo, chickens, scooters and songthaews share them.

## The rental desk 🔑 (`rental`; buckle up, keep left)

One short beat, not a full activity (about 30-45 s, 6-10 touches).

- A small arrivals hall, a rental counter with a smiling **clerk**. The clerk slides a big key across the counter.
- **The toy:** the key fob is in her hand. Press it and the big Pajero outside beeps and flashes its lights. Press
  again and again: beep-beep, the lights wink, the mirrors fold out, Daddy jumps.
- **Then:** drag the suitcase into the boot (it thumps in and the car squats on its springs). Kaylee hops into her seat
  behind Mommy; tap the seatbelt and it **clicks**. Mommy at the wheel on the right: "Ready? Let's go!"
- **Pokeables:** the clerk's bell, a rack of maps, a pot of orchids, a poster of a mountain that rains when tapped.
- **Teaches by doing:** buckle up before the car goes (it waits for the click); the wheel is on the right because cars
  in Thailand keep to the left.

## The mountain road 🚙 (`drive`; keep left, slow on bends, clouds, counting)

**One-line pitch:** Mommy drives the big Pajero through the mountains; Kaylee steers it round the bends, beeps at the
blind corners and drives right up into the clouds.

**The toy (core verb):** steer the Pajero along a swoopy mountain road with her finger. It always rolls forward
(Mommy's foot); her finger, anywhere on the road ahead, picks where across the road it goes. Let go and Mommy keeps it
in the lane by herself.
- How it feels: heavy and bouncy. The big body leans out on the bends and squats when it brakes. The wipers swish, the
  tyres hiss in puddles, and the road climbs and dips so the view swings.
- Why it's fun with no goal: driving a big car round curves is fun on its own, and every bend reveals something new.
  Greybox test: a box car on a ribbon road over grey hills, three road shapes, the horn.

**How the verb deepens (three roads, same verb):**
1. **`valley`** (to the mushroom farm, in Pua): a gentle road through rice fields. Buffalo graze, chickens
   cross, a **songthaew passes on the other side**: steer onto the right side and Mommy says "Keep left!" and eases
   back. A buffalo steps onto the road and the Pajero slows by itself until it ambles across.
2. **`sky`** (to Sapan and Bo Kluea, over Doi Phu Kha: the café and the salt wells): steep S-bends climbing into the cloud. The cloud gets thicker until
   it's white all round (the headlights come on), then the Pajero pops out above it into sunshine. At **blind bends**
   a round mirror shows a car coming: tap the horn (the steering-wheel boss, in the car) for a short beep before the
   bend, and the other car beeps back.
3. **`three`** (the way home, from the last stop): the hairpins down to Santi Suk. A chevron sign on each bend. Kaylee
   counts "one… two… three" bends aloud with Sparkle, then at the viewpoint the camera lifts up and the road below
   draws a giant **3** (the `three` section).

To the temple is a short city road (`city`): tuk-tuks, a market, the river bridge, 20-30 s.

Side verb: **the horn** (one tap). The wipers come on by themselves in the rain, but tapping them makes them swish
faster.

**Her choices:** where on the road (hug the mountain side or the valley side), when to beep, which stop next (the map).

**Pokeables:** the buffalo (moo), the chickens (flap off the road), scooters with families waving, a monkey on the
guard rail, a waterfall by the road, the mirror, the dashboard toy (a nodding elephant).

**Silly moments (instead of failure):** steer at the guard rail and it boings the Pajero back like a pinball bumper
(Daddy's "Whoa!"); keep beeping and a buffalo moos back; drive onto the right side and the songthaew driver waves and
honks a tune. The car can't leave the road and there's no crash.

**Story frame:** Mommy: "Let's drive to the mushroom farm! Steer for me, Kaylee!" (first drive only). Sparkle speaks
when something new appears: the first blind bend and mirror ("Beep before the bend!"), the first cloud ("We're
driving into a cloud!"), the hairpins ("Let's count the bends!"), or after 8 s with the finger off and nothing
touched ("Steer the car with your finger!").

**What it teaches, by doing:** keep left (the songthaew on the other side), go slow on bends (the Pajero slows itself
before each chevron sign and leans), beep at blind bends, clouds sit on mountains (she drives into one), count to
three (the hairpins). Tap-to-hear facts: the Pajero is made in Thailand; in Thailand cars drive on the left; the big
round mirrors show cars coming round the bend.

**Payoff:** the stop at the end of the road (the Pajero pulls in and everyone hops out), and on the way home, the 3.

**Replay:** the roads are the same, the traffic and animals are random; the weather (rain, sun, cloud) changes.

**Numbers:** each drive about 45-75 s with 15-30 touches (holding and steering counts as continuous play, plus the
horn and pokeables). About 4-5 drives in a full trip.

**Camera:** a chase camera behind and above the Pajero, looking ahead along the road (the race's `Shot` with
look-ahead), lifting higher on hairpins so she can see the bend coming. In portrait it sits higher to show more road.
`three` ends with a slow crane up and over to the viewpoint, framing the whole 3.

**Tech:** a road kit in `trip/road/` (reusable for any later drive): the road is a list of points with heights in
meters (pure data), turned into an open spline; the ribbon road, the verges, the hills either side and the guard rails
are built from it. The race's `spotAt`/`locate` maths, extended to height. Clouds are a few big soft toon blobs and
the particle pool (mist). The Pajero runs on `Driver` with a heavy `Feel` (low top speed, strong lean). **Riskiest:**
steering on a road that climbs and turns at the same time, with a camera that never loses the next bend, on an
iPhone in portrait.

## The stops (each `../ACTIVITY-TEMPLATE.md` in short)

Each follows the template: one core verb that deepens three times, about 20-40 touches and 1.5-3 minutes, at most one
or two spoken lines before her hands are busy, no failing. Each stop ends with a family photo and a stamp on the map.

### 1. Wat Phumin 🛕 (`temple`; the wai, a temple from long ago, four directions)

- **Pitch:** the old temple's paintings are asleep in the dim hall. Kaylee's lantern wakes them up.
- **Arriving:** shoes off at the steps (she taps each shoe and it pops onto the rack: the lesson by doing), up the naga
  stairs between the two big serpents (tap a naga and it blinks and its tail wiggles; sacred things never wobble, but
  the naga is a guardian, and it only blinks).
- **The toy:** drag a lantern over the dark walls of the hall. Where its glow falls, the painting wakes up: painted
  people wave, boats paddle, an elephant flaps its ears, birds fly from one picture to the next.
- **How it deepens:**
  1. Light anything: the walls are full of little scenes that come alive.
  2. **Find the whispering lovers:** a soft whisper sound gets louder as the lantern gets near; when lit, the man
     whispers into the woman's ear and little hearts float up. (Sparkle: "What's he whispering? Shh!")
  3. **The four Buddhas:** the four doors open one by one with light; she walks round and at each golden Buddha her
     unicorn does a **wai**, a candle glows and a bell rings. Four directions, four bells.
- **Her choices:** which wall to light, which story to wake.
- **Pokeables:** the painted elephant, a painted boat race (the boats race along the wall), the pigeons in the
  courtyard, the bell, the lovers' statues in the garden (they whisper too).
- **Silly:** shine the lantern on Daddy and a painted Daddy appears in the mural, waving; light the same scene three
  times and its people dance.
- **Teaches:** take shoes off and wai in a temple; Wat Phumin is very old; the four Buddhas face the four directions.
  Facts: the temple sits on two giant naga serpents; the whispering lovers are Nan's most famous painting.
- **Payoff:** the whole hall glows gold with every scene awake; the family photo under the lovers.
- **Camera:** inside the hall, a slow orbit round the four Buddhas; the walls are the stage.

### 2. Baan Huanam Mushroom Farm 🍄 (`mushrooms`; mushrooms aren't plants, they love dark and wet)

- **Pitch:** the farmer's mushroom house is thirsty. Kaylee mists the bags and the mushrooms puff up, then she picks
  the best ones for lunch.
- **The toy:** hold the misting hose over the rows of sawdust bags. A soft spray hisses out where her finger is; a
  rainbow shows in the mist. Under the spray, tiny mushrooms pop out of the bags' mouths and swell into frilly fans.
- **How it deepens:**
  1. **Mist:** grey oyster mushrooms pop and grow wherever she sprays. The dark house drips and glistens.
  2. **Pick:** ready mushrooms wiggle; drag one and twist and it comes off into the basket with a pop. Keep spraying
     one and it grows enormous, bouncy and silly (she can boing on it).
  3. **Pink and gold:** new bags sprout **pink oyster mushrooms** and golden ones; she picks a basket of colors.
- **Side verb:** at the farm's table, put her basket on the **mushroom pizza** (place-anywhere toppings, like a
  sticker board on the pizza), then it comes out of the oven and the family shares it with a view.
- **Her choices:** which bags to mist, which mushrooms (and colors) to pick, where they go on the pizza.
- **Pokeables:** the farmer (cast: a farmer aunty) who laughs, a gecko on the wall, a sleepy dog, the drip from the roof,
  the little sign of a mushroom drawing (a mushroom with a smiley face).
- **Silly:** spray Daddy and he gets a mushroom hat; the giant mushroom she bounces on.
- **Teaches:** mushrooms don't grow from seeds in soil like plants: they grow from sawdust in a dark, damp house, and
  they love the rainy season. Facts: mushrooms aren't plants; Baan Huanam grows its own mushrooms; pink oyster
  mushrooms are real.
- **Payoff:** the full basket, then the pizza with her mushrooms on it.
- **Carried state:** `basket` (e.g. `pink2,grey5,gold1`) goes to the pizza.
- **Camera:** inside the mushroom house, a low side view along the rows of bags; the pizza straight on at the table.

### 3. Stop Time Cafe, Sapan ☕ (`rice`; rice grows in water, terraces hold water)

- **Pitch:** the rice steps below the café, down in Sapan village, are dry. Kaylee brings the water down, step by step, and the valley turns
  shiny and green.
- **The toy:** a bamboo pipe brings water from the stream at the top. She drags its end; wherever it points, water
  pours. The top paddy fills, goes shiny like a mirror, then spills over a notch into the next step down, and the next.
- **How it deepens:**
  1. **Fill the steps:** each filled paddy shines and reflects the sky; frogs jump in, ducks paddle in.
  2. **Plant:** a bundle of green seedlings floats by each shiny step; push them into the mud one by one (plip!), and
     they grow green in a few seconds. A farmer and his buffalo plough the next step.
  3. **Clouds roll in:** big soft clouds cover the valley; she wipes them away with her finger to see the village and
     the green steps again (like the plane's clouds), and a rainbow comes out.
- **Her choices:** which step to fill first, where to plant.
- **Pokeables:** frogs, ducks, the buffalo, a farmer's hat on a post, the café's wind chime, rain dripping off the roof.
- **Silly:** pour the water on the café deck and Daddy's coffee overflows; pour on the buffalo and it has a happy bath.
- **Teaches:** rice grows standing in water; the steps (terraces) keep the water on a hill; June is planting time.
- **Payoff:** the whole valley green and shiny; then Kaylee and Daddy sit on the tall stools at the deck railing
  (recreating photo 5), she drinks a **pink milk** (นมเย็น, nom yen), and Mommy takes the photo.
- **Camera:** from the café's balcony on the hill, looking down over Sapan's valley (the steps fill the safe zone); then the deck close-up
  for the photo.

### 4. Bo Kluea salt wells 🧂 (`salt`; salt from the mountains, boiling)

- **Pitch:** in a village high in the mountains, there's a well full of very salty water. Kaylee helps the salt aunty
  turn it into white salt.
- **The toy:** a bucket on a long bamboo pole over the well. Drag it down into the well (splash, glug), lift it up full
  and swing it over the big pan, tip it and the brine pours in.
- **How it deepens:**
  1. **Dip and pour:** fill the big pan bucket by bucket (it fills up visibly).
  2. **Fire:** tap the logs under the pan (or blow, holding a finger on the fire) and the flames grow; steam rises in
     clouds; the water level drops.
  3. **Salt!:** white crystals appear at the edges and spread; she scrapes them with the wooden paddle into the
     basket. The basket fills up, heaped and sparkling, and the salt aunty pours it into bags on the shelf.
- **Her choices:** when to pour, how big the fire.
- **Pokeables:** the salt aunty (laughs), a cat sleeping warm by the fire, the drying baskets, a rooster, the mural of
  the Tai Lue couple on the house across the road (from Dad's video).
- **Silly:** she licks a finger: a big salty face (her unicorn's mouth puckers, eyes squeeze); pour brine on Daddy's
  foot and he hops.
- **Teaches:** there's salt in the mountains, far from the sea, because a sea was here long, long ago; boiling the
  water away leaves the salt behind. Facts: people in Bo Kluea have made salt this way for hundreds of years.
- **Payoff:** a heaped basket of white salt she made, and a shelf of full bags.
- **Camera:** inside the open-sided salt hut, side view: the well on the left, the pan and fire on the right.

### The "3" road (`three`; counting)

The last drive (`drive?road=three`), and then the viewpoint: the Pajero stops at a lay-by, the camera lifts up and
over the hill, and the road below is a big swoopy **3**. Kaylee traces it with her finger and a crayon line follows
(one, two, three), the family photo is taken with the 3 behind them, and Sparkle says "Three! Kaylee drove the 3!".
Then the road goes on to the airport and the plane home.

## Home 🏠

Agong waves hello, and the photo she picks (the café balcony, the 3, the mushroom pizza, the lovers) goes on his wall.
Tap it and Mommy says "Our trip to Nan!".

Params:
