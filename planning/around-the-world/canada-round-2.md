# 🍁 Canada, round two: three chapters rebuilt on what she replays (a plan, not built)

**Art direction.** Use the released Maple's Team activities and films as the visual reference. Build with Canada's
existing 3D rigs, sets, and toon shading. The generated JPGs in `canada-round-2/` are archived pitch illustrations.
Ignore them when designing scenes, characters, and gameplay visuals.

**The pitch:** Maple's Team keeps its story, its map, its films and its big game. Three of its six chapters are
rebuilt as the kind of toy she goes back to: she gives a muddy bear a **bubble bath and a new look**, plays
**flashlight statues** with a sneaky raccoon, and plays **peek-a-boo with whales** to get an otter's lucky rock back.
Each friend now has a small trouble that her play fixes, the fix is the talent the team needs, and what she makes
goes with the team to the big game. A fourth, optional piece puts her favorite toy of all, the wardrobe, at the
rink: a cabin where she dresses the team.

1. **Misty's Bubble Bath** (replaces Salmon Leap)
2. **Campfire Night, in 3D: Flashlight Statues**
3. **Whale Peek-a-Boo** (Whale Watch with a reason)
4. **The Team Cabin** (optional, after the three)

Three more threads tie it together:

- **Silly faces, in close-up.** The laugh she repeats in Koh Samet is one small trick: crossed eyes swapped onto
  Dad's face, a wiggle and a tight camera. Canada gets it as a kit for its whole cast (soaked, dazzled by the light,
  sudsy, dizzy), and each rebuilt toy has one as its big moment: Maple soaked by a whale, Roxy frozen in the beam,
  Misty seeing herself in the mirror.
- **Gus hears about it.** Each time a friend joins, Gus says one line at the rink as she lands back on the map, and
  he brags a little less each time ("One friend? Honk! Easy!" ... "A whole team? Honk... gulp."). It comes after
  Sparkle's count without holding her hands, works in any order (it goes by how many, not who), and makes the big
  game something that has been coming all along.
- **The new friend shows their move.** When she lands back on the map with a new friend, the friend pops in at the
  rink as today, then does their move once beside the ice, about 2 s, with no words: Tuque bounces a puck off his
  tummy, Monty stands like a statue while two geese wobble past him, Roxy whisks a puck from a goose, Lulu sings a
  few notes that drift up over the canal, Misty winds up and sends a pinecone over the boards, Ollie juggles her rock
  and spin-passes it. Then Gus's line. Tapping any friend already on the team at the rink plays their move again. The
  team she has brought home is something she can watch, not just count, and in the big game each friend's move starts
  with the same wind-up, so she knows who is about to do what.
- **What she makes stays.** Misty plays the big game in the look she was given, in a team sweater with her own paw
  print on it. With the cabin (part 4), so does everybody.

## 1. Misty's Bubble Bath 🛁 (replaces Salmon Leap; 3D; the rainforest river)

**One line:** Misty the spirit bear is covered in mud; she rubs her clean, fluffs her up and paints her with berries,
and Misty goes to the big game looking like that.

- **Her finger:** rub the bear. Whatever tool is in hand works on the fur under her finger. A tap with a tool does a
  short go with it, so she never has to drag. The first tool is already in hand: play starts on the first touch.
- **Why it's fun with no goal:** suds pile up where she rubs and can be pushed into shapes (a mohawk, horns, a
  beard), and the bear giggles.
- **Grows three times, same verb:**
  1. 🧽 **Wash.** The sponge leaves pink suds and the mud comes off under them. Then the rain cloud is in her hand:
     rain falls where her finger is and rinses. White shows through. Sparkle, at that moment: "A spirit bear! A
     black bear with white fur."
  2. 🌬️ **Fluff.** The towel: where she rubs, the fur puffs. Rub a lot and Misty is a giant puffball ("I'm a
     cloud!"). The comb: stroke up for spikes, down for bangs, sideways for a swoop.
  3. 🎨 **Colors.** A white bear is a blank page. Three berry bushes (salmonberry pink, blueberry blue, huckleberry
     purple): squash one and rub, and the fur takes the color, as much or as little as she likes. Then ferns,
     flowers and bows from the mossy log go on wherever she drops them (the side verb).
- **The end:** after enough strokes in part 3 the still pool glows like a mirror. She taps it: Misty sees herself,
  the camera goes close, and she pulls the big face. Then Maple holds up a white team sweater with the red maple
  leaf on it. One tap on the sweater and Misty presses her paw onto it, THUMP, leaving a print in whatever berry
  colors are on that paw (a paw she didn't paint leaves a plain white one). Misty pulls the sweater on, and the
  closing film plays in her look. (This idea came from the sweater shop pitch in PR #501. The sweater is one press,
  not a fourth stage: the decorations are already the bath's one side verb.)
- **Her choices:** which color where, how much fluff, which hairdo, what goes on her head. None is wrong.
- **Silly:** tap Misty's nose and she sneezes suds over everything. Tickle her tummy and she kicks the water. Rub
  suds on Maple and Maple has a foam beard. Drop the soap near Misty and she bats it clean over the cedars (Maple:
  "What a shot!"): the slap shot, before anyone says hockey. A wet Misty shakes like a dog and soaks Maple (soaked
  face). Tap the tall cedar and Misty scratches her back on it and does a shimmy.
- **Never fails:** no clean meter she can see. A muddy patch she's left sparkles after 8 s; the rain finishes any
  last bits by itself. Any amount of color is enough.
- **Pokeables:** the salmon jumping up the falls (tap: it leaps; Misty swings, misses, and the splash rinses her:
  "Missed again!"), the cedar, a banana slug, the raven, the berry bushes, the falls, Maple, the rain cloud, the
  soap.
- **Learns by doing:** a spirit bear is a black bear with white fur (she uncovers it); it rains a lot in a rainforest
  (the rain is her rinse); bears eat berries and salmon (the berries are her paint, the salmon are in the falls).
- **Stays:** the look and the sweater print are saved. Misty wears them in her closing film, on the map, at the
  rink and in the big game.
  Next visit she is muddy again and gets a new one.
- **Numbers:** about 2.5-3 min, 40-60 strokes and taps, 1 spoken line before her hands are busy.
- **Sections:** `story`, `wash`, `fluff`, `colors`, `mirror` (the mirror, then the sweater). The look rides in the
  link (`?look=`), with a default.

## 2. Campfire Night, in 3D: Flashlight Statues ⛺

**One line:** somebody is taking Maple's marshmallows in the dark; her finger is the flashlight, and whoever it
lands on freezes with a silly face.

- **Her finger:** where it goes, the light goes. One 3D clearing at night; what's in the dark is a black shape with
  two shining eyes.
- **Grows three times, same verb:**
  1. 👀 **Who's out there?** Whatever the light lands on does its bit at once, no waiting: the owl's head goes all
     the way round, the porcupine puffs into a ball, the deer freezes with a mouthful of leaves, the frog's throat
     balloons, the bat yawns upside down, Monty squints ("Whoa! Too bright!") and puts on sunglasses. Any order, no
     count. After three or four of them, giggling starts in the dark.
  2. 🙈 **Peek-a-boo.** Roxy's three kits peek out from behind a trunk, the tent, a log, and duck when the light
     swings their way. Catch one in the beam: it freezes in a pose, then tumbles out giggling and runs to the fire.
     Each peek lasts longer than the one before.
  3. 🦝 **Statues.** Roxy sneaks toward the marshmallow bag whenever the light is off her, and freezes whenever it's
     on her, in a new ridiculous pose each time ("I'm a tree." "I'm a rock." "Nobody here!"). Hold the light on her
     and she wobbles, then falls over laughing. Three of those and she gives up: "Oops, sorry! Let's share."
- **Side verb, kept from today:** roast a marshmallow (white, golden, on fire: tap to blow it out) and hand it to
  anyone. Maple likes golden, Roxy loves burnt, Monty eats the stick.
- **The end:** "Now tap the flashlight to turn it off!" Dark. The stars come out, then the northern lights. (Today's
  ending shines the light at the sky to bring out the stars, which is backwards.)
- **Silly:** the poses. If she leaves Roxy alone, Roxy reaches the bag, takes one ("Yoink!") and scampers back to try
  again: funny, and the game goes on. Light on Maple: she makes shadow bunnies with her paws. Light on the fire
  for a while: nothing happens and Maple says "It's already bright!"
- **Never fails:** nothing has to be caught quickly. After 8 s a kit's tail sticks out and wiggles; Roxy slows down
  after each grab.
- **Learns by doing:** night animals' eyes shine back at a light; who is awake at night; raccoons have clever
  little hands; you see the stars when it's dark; the northern lights dance over Canada.
- **Numbers:** about 2.5-3 min; the light is moving nearly all of it, plus about 25 taps and holds.
- **Sections:** `story`, `eyes`, `peek`, `statues`, `roast`, `sky`.
- **Gone:** the sounds round (left or right by ear on iPad speakers), the paw-print trail, the flat animal pictures.

## 3. Whale Peek-a-Boo 🐋 (Whale Watch with a reason)

**One line:** a whale's spout blew Ollie's lucky rock out to sea; the whales come up to the boat one by one, she
plays with each, and the baby orca has the rock.

- **The reason (a real one):** every sea otter has a favorite rock, keeps it in a pocket of skin under her arm, and
  juggles it. That's why Ollie has the fastest pass. The opening film shows her juggling it, higher and higher,
  until a whale comes up underneath.
- **Her finger:** touch the bubbles and a whale comes up right there: peek-a-boo. Then the whale is the toy.
- **Grows three times, same verb:**
  1. 🐋 **The grey whale** (gentle). Tap the blowhole: a spout, all over Maple (soaked face, a different one each
     time). Rub its back: it rolls over and waves a flipper. Tap the barnacles: it laughs bubbles. Ollie asks about
     the rock; it lifts its tail and there's a sea star on it.
  2. 🐳 **The humpback** (big). Press and hold: it sinks and the bubbles grow. Let go: it jumps. A longer hold is a
     bigger jump and a bigger wave, and Maple's face gets sillier with the size. The longest hold lands the boat on
     the whale's back for a short ride.
  3. 🖤 **The orcas.** The baby pops straight up beside the boat with the rock on its nose (orcas really do this to
     look around), here, then there, giggling. Tap it and it tosses the rock high and catches it. Each pop lasts
     longer; the third stays until she taps. Then Grandma comes up, one look, and the baby flicks the rock to Ollie.
     All the whales jump together.
- **The boat:** still goes where she holds the water (it works, and it's the canoe's control), but nothing is far
  away and nothing needs steering: left alone, each whale comes to her. The sail out of the harbour is in the film.
- **Silly:** the soaked faces; tap Ollie and she spins; tap the microphone and the humpback sings into it; hold the
  grey whale's tail and it gives the boat a little push.
- **Never fails:** every tap and hold does something; the baby's pops only get easier.
- **Learns by doing:** whales breathe air out of the top of their heads (she taps it); humpbacks jump and sing; orca
  families follow the grandma; otters use a rock as a tool and keep a favorite.
- **Numbers:** about 2.5 min, about 35 touches.
- **Sections:** `story`, `grey`, `humpback`, `orcas`.
- **Gone:** steer out past the lighthouse, creep up on sleeping Ollie and tow her home, chase four spouts, copy the
  orcas' turns, the listening section (the microphone stays as something to tap).

## 4. The Team Cabin 🧣 (optional, after the three; on the map at the rink)

Her most-played toy is the wardrobe. A team needs uniforms. So the skate hut beside the canal opens: the friends
who have joined sit on the bench, and she dresses them.

- **Her finger:** drag a thing onto a friend, or tap it and it flies on. Jerseys (5 colors), hats (pom-pom hat,
  helmet, crown, flower crown, antler headband, chef's hat), scarves (5), a cheek stamp (leaf, heart, star).
- **Grows by itself:** it opens with Maple alone. Each chapter adds a friend to the bench, so there's a reason to go
  back after every stamp, six times.
- **Silly:** things land wherever she drops them (a skate on Monty's antler, then it hops down). Each friend has a
  line for a hat they love. Gus pokes his head in at the window: he can have a hat too ("Honk! ...I look good.").
- **Stays:** on the map, at the rink, in the films at the rink and in the big game. Misty's berry colors from her
  bath show here too.
- **No stamp, no end:** a toy on the map, like Maple's animal book. The mirror takes a team photo.

## 1. Misty's Bubble Bath: build notes

- **World:** a new 3D activity, `activities/bath/`. Start from the film set `story/sets/salmon.ts` (the rainforest
  river: cedars, falls, rain; 453 lines) so the game and its films are one place; rename it `sets/bath.ts`.
- **Misty:** the rig friend from `characters/looks.ts` (`s.cast.misty` in films). She sits in the pool, facing the
  camera, big: her body is most of the screen, so every zone is a target far over 64 px.
- **Mud, suds, color (the riskiest piece: prototype it first, alone, with a robot).** Suggested: the rig's body
  parts are zones (head, each ear, muzzle, chest, tummy, back, arms, legs). Each zone holds `mud 0-1`, `puff 0-1`
  and a color; rubbing a zone with a tool moves those and lerps its material. Suds are instanced blobs spawned at
  the ray hit, parented to the nearest bone, that stack along the stroke and can be pushed; rain removes them.
  A canvas-painted fur texture is the richer option if the rig's UVs allow it; decide after an hour with each.
- **Her hairdo:** the barber's `Mane` (`mall-trip/games/barber/mane.ts`, `hair.ts`: 26 tufts of instanced strands,
  two draw calls; comb for spikes or flat, dryer, color spray, bows) is what she already loves. Move it into
  `engine3d/characters/` if it comes away cleanly (the Salon then imports it from there) and give Misty a topknot and
  cheek tufts built with it. If it doesn't, a handful of tuft meshes with three shapes each is enough.
- **Tools** sit on a mossy log at the bottom edge, big, at most three showing at once; the one in hand glows. The
  next tool arrives when the part is done enough (mud under about 20%; about 8 strokes with the towel and comb,
  like the barber's `ENOUGH = 8`), with one Sparkle line.
- **Decorations** drop onto the nearest of the rig's `spots` (crown, hat, nose, ears) or stick where they land.
- **Feel knobs** (`knobs('bath')`, `?feel=1`): suds per stroke, mud per stroke, puff per stroke, how far a tap's
  short go reaches, how long before the pool glows.
- **The sweater:** the team sweater is a prop from `story/props.ts` with the maple leaf, held by Maple. The print is
  a decal of the paw-pad shape tinted with the paw zone's colors (two or three colors if she painted it in stripes),
  put at a fixed spot on the chest so it reads at map size. Saved in `MistyLook.print`; the tap target is the whole
  sweater (well over 88 px).
- **The look:** `MistyLook = { fur: zone colors, puff, hair: style + color, worn: ids, print: paw colors }`, saved through
  `canada/looks.ts`, applied by one `dressMisty(rig, look)` used by the game, the film cast, the map and the rink.
- **Pacing:** Sparkle speaks when a tool arrives and after 8 s stuck; Misty and Maple react at most every 12 s
  (the barber's `SILLY_GAP`), never after every stroke.
- **Delete:** `activities/salmon/` (SalmonLeap.tsx, fish.ts, stage.ts, Bear.tsx, noises.ts, sections.ts, qa.mjs),
  `story/films/salmon.ts` → rewritten as `films/bath.ts`, the `salmon-*.webp` scene art, `cover-game-salmon.webp`
  (a new cover and shelf icon for the bath, per `docs/art.md` and `src/world/shelf/STYLE.md`). Keep
  `animal-salmon.webp` (the animal book) and the Theater video.
