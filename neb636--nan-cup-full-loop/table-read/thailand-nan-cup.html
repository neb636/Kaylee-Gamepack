The Nan Mountain Cup (activities/nan-trip/cup/, plan: thailand/nan-cup.md): a kart race on three mountain tracks against three Nan locals (cast.json): Grandma Taeng the tortoise, Pao the hornbill postman and Moon the sun bear honey seller. Sparkle says what's new and counts down in Thai; the rivals tease, bump and are good sports in their own voices; Mommy and Daddy cheer from the grandstand. Kaylee never talks: she giggles.
